## 0. Executive summary

- **The 2023–2025 FA downturn is over, and an AI/semiconductor capex upcycle is under way.** Every company reported accelerating orders in Apr–Sep 2026:
  - Mitsubishi Electric FA orders were up 42% YoY in Q1 FY3/27 [R].
  - Yaskawa's Q2 (Jun–Aug 2026) orders were a record, up 38% YoY [R].
  - Omron raised its FY3/27 operating-profit guidance by 29%, from ¥62bn to ¥80bn [R].
  - Fanuc raised FY3/27 sales guidance to ¥1.01tn, its first year above ¥1tn [R].
- **Profit pool ranking (latest fiscal year):** Keyence's operating profit is about 3× Fanuc's.
  - Keyence: OP ¥596bn on a 51% margin.
  - Fanuc: OP ¥184bn on a 21.4% margin.
  - Mitsubishi Electric FA Systems: OP ¥75bn on 9.4%, guided to ¥120bn and 13.3%.
  - Omron IAB: OP ¥43bn on 10.4%.
  - Yaskawa: OP ¥47bn on 8.7%.
  - Inovance: net profit CNY 5.05bn on revenue of CNY 45.1bn (2025).
- **The structural threat comes from China.** Inovance holds about 30% of China's general-purpose servo market, more than 4× Yaskawa's ~6%. Domestic brands now hold about 60% of China servo and about 37% of small PLCs [R, industry white paper]. Estun out-shipped foreign brands in China robots by Q1 2025 [R]. Japanese vendors are keeping China revenue through the upcycle (AI, semiconductors, EV, humanoid parts), but are losing share in mid-range servo, PLC and robots.
- **Downturn resilience ranking:** Keyence > Fanuc >> Mitsubishi Electric (diversified group) > Inovance (cost and scale, but margins are being squeezed) > Yaskawa ≈ Omron.
  - Keyence and Fanuc have huge net cash. Fanuc's cash alone is ¥615bn.
  - Omron and Yaskawa have thin margins and needed restructuring in this cycle.
- **People view:** Keyence pays the most of any listed Japanese company of its size (average ~¥21.8m, FY2022), but Japanese-language sales culture dominates. Omron cut ~2,000 jobs (NEXT 2025) and is now in growth mode. Mitsubishi Electric drew ~4,700 early-retirement applicants (age 53+) at a cost of ~¥100bn in FY3/26. Strategy roles for non-Japanese speakers sit mainly in the US and EU subsidiaries, not at Japanese HQs. The exception is Fanuc, which is fully centralized in Yamanashi.

- Cost-down pressure is high, but Fanuc's brand and reliability support its share in high-end lines (auto, high-mix).

### e) Resilience

High. Fanuc has a net-cash fortress, a 17–25% margin floor through cycles, and a service annuity worth 16% of sales.

### d) Technology and competition

- Strong in vision, measurement and microscopes. In China, the threats are Hikrobot (Hikvision's machine-vision unit) and Luster/OPT in vision.
- Keyence's moat is its consultative direct sales and speed of delivery, which are hard for Chinese rivals to copy outside China.

### e) Resilience

Highest in the sector: a 50%+ margin at the cycle trough, a fabless asset-light model, and a massive cash buffer.
