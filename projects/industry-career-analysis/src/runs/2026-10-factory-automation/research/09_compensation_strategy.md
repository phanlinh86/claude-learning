# 09 — Compensation: Corporate Strategy / Corp Dev / Strategic Planning in Factory Automation (global)

*Analyst note, as of 28 Oct 2026. Reader profile: about 15 years in corporate strategy (Senior Manager, Director, Head of Strategy, VP Strategy). This is not financial or career advice. Figures without a source carry an [EST] label.*

**Source tags:** [LCA] H-1B Labor Condition Application filing · [SR] self-reported (Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, Blind, PayScale, StepStone, Kununu, OpenWork) · [POST] posted pay range (pay-transparency law) · [GOV] government statistics or statutory securities filing (yuho) · [REC] recruiter salary guide · [EST] analyst estimate or derivation.

**FX used (EST, late 2026, approximate):** EUR 1 = USD 1.15; CHF 1 = USD 1.24; USD 1 = JPY 150; SGD 1 = USD 0.77; USD 1 = CNY 7.1.

**Search budget:** 21 web searches were used. The gaps are listed in Section 9.

## 0. Bottom line

1. **US industrial automation majors pay a 15-year strategy professional roughly USD 250–400k total direct compensation (TDC) at Director level.** That breaks down as base USD 190–260k, a 25–35% bonus target and LTI worth about 25–60% of base. At VP or Head of Strategy level, TDC is roughly USD 450–900k. Posted director ranges at Siemens US were USD 169–304k and USD 191–328k base, plus a 25% target bonus [POST]. Glassdoor's US "Director of Strategy" median is about USD 247k total pay [SR].
2. **The same seniority pays roughly 2–3x more at big tech, MBB consulting and PE.** Amazon L8 director TDC averages about USD 830k [SR], MBB Associate Partner about USD 320–480k cash [REC], and PE Principal about USD 470–940k cash before carry [survey]. In exchange, industrial strategy roles offer more stable hours, lower layoff risk and an operating-leadership exit path (Business Unit GM).
3. **Europe pays 20–40% less in cash than the US.** Switzerland (Zurich, ABB) is the exception and is close to US cash levels. In Germany, every strategy role at this seniority sits **above the collective agreement** (*außertariflich*, AT, i.e. individually contracted), above roughly EUR 100–110k. A Siemens or Schneider Director-level strategy role is about EUR 150–230k TDC [EST].
4. **Japan has a flat pay curve, with Keyence the outlier.** Keyence's statutory average is JPY 20.39M (FY3/2025) at an average age of 34.8 [GOV]. A corporate-planning *bucho* (department head) in Japan averages about JPY 11.8M, and a division head (*honbucho*) or above about JPY 15.4M [REC]. Japanese offices of foreign firms pay about the same on average, but more at the top end.
5. **Singapore (APAC HQ) is the best-paid location in Asia.** Director-level strategy, M&A or corporate-development roles earn SGD 230–350k (USD 180–270k) [REC], with a low income tax rate. In Shanghai, multinational strategy directors earn about CNY 0.6–1.2M [SR/POST]. Inovance pays strong cash but limited LTI to non-core staff [EST].
6. **Equity tailwind.** ABB (all-time high in April 2026), Schneider (all-time high of EUR 288.80 in June 2026, then about EUR 300 in September) and Siemens (Xetra high of EUR 289.45 in August 2026, +14% YTD to early October) all rose strongly through 2024–26 on data-center electrification demand. LTI grants made in 2023–24 are vesting well above grant value, which lifts *realized* director TDC by about 10–25% versus target [EST]. Schneider fell about 20% from its September high in October 2026, so 2026–27 grants carry more downside.

### 1d. Synthesized US ladder for industrial automation majors [EST]

Built from postings, LCAs, Glassdoor and proxy-statement norms.

| Level (typical for 15 yrs exp.) | Base (USD) | Bonus target | LTI (annual grant value) | TDC at target (USD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Senior Manager, Strategy / Corp Dev | 160–200k | 15–20% | 0–15% of base (often none or RSUs) | 185–250k |
| **Director, Strategy / Corp Dev** | **190–260k** | **25–30%** | **20–50% of base** (RSUs/PSUs; Siemens US uses Siemens Stock Awards) | **260–400k** |
| Senior Director / Head of Strategy (Business Unit or segment) | 240–310k | 30–40% | 50–100% | 400–600k |
| VP Strategy / Corp Dev (enterprise; Section 16 officer at Rockwell or Emerson level) | 300–425k | 50–60% | 100–200% | 600k–1.2M |

*Bonus and LTI norms, director level:* Short-term incentive (STI) targets of 20–30% of base are typical across Rockwell, Emerson, Honeywell, Siemens US, ABB Inc. and Schneider USA. Realized payouts were about 70–130% of target over 2024–26, reflecting a soft 2024 automation demand trough and a 2025–26 recovery. US-listed firms usually grant LTI in 3-year vesting tranches, mixing RSUs and PSUs, with a stronger PSU tilt at VP level. US subsidiaries of European parents (Siemens, ABB, Schneider) typically run smaller LTI at director level than US-listed peers, but often offer a share-matching plan. Japanese subsidiaries (Mitsubishi Electric US, Omron, Keyence Corp. of America) generally give **no meaningful LTI** to non-executives [EST].

*Keyence Corp. of America:* Its pay is weighted toward sales and its corporate strategy function is very small. Most strategy is run from Osaka HQ, so the US entity offers few strategy roles at this level [EST].

## 3. Europe (Germany, Switzerland, France)

### 3a. Germany: collective-agreement bands vs AT

- Siemens, ABB Germany and Schneider Germany are bound by the **IG Metall metal & electrical collective agreement (Tarifvertrag)**. That agreement's top pay groups (ERA EG 17 and above in Bavaria) reach roughly EUR 85–100k in base pay before supplements [EST]. Strategy and M&A professionals with 8+ years are almost always **AT (*außertariflich*, i.e. individually contracted above the collective agreement)**: there is no overtime pay, the bonus is negotiated individually, and the salary must contractually exceed the top collective-agreement group by a margin.
- Siemens AG grades its leaders as "Leading Executives" and "Senior Management". From mid-management upward these grades include **Siemens Stock Awards / PSUs** (performance-based share awards) and the **Share Matching Plan** (employee share purchase with matching shares).

| Level (Munich / Erlangen) | Base (EUR) | Bonus | LTI | TDC (EUR) | TDC (USD) | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| StepStone "Director of Strategy", Munich (all firms) | Median 96.1k (88–113k) | — | — | — | about 110k | [SR] (likely understates large caps) |
| Senior Manager / Senior Strategy Consultant, AT | 105–135k | 10–20% | Share matching | 115–160k | 130–185k | [EST] |
| **Director / Head of Strategy (BU or Division), AT / Leading Executive** | **130–175k** | **20–35%** | **15–40% of base** | **160–240k** | **185–275k** | [EST] |

**Career implications for the reader:**
- **Highest risk-adjusted cash in the sector:** ABB in Zurich and US-based Director or VP roles at Rockwell, Emerson or Honeywell.
- **Highest absolute upside:** these roles do not offer it. Big tech, PE and MBB all pay more.
- **Japan:** attractive only at Keyence (where strategy roles are rare) or in a foreign firm's Japan office.
- **Singapore:** the best Asian base for an APAC strategy-head role.
- **Promotion economics:** the largest pay jump in industrials is from Director to VP or Business Unit GM, where TDC roughly doubles through LTI. Staying in a staff-strategy role usually tops out at Senior Director.

## 8. Methodology notes

- An LCA shows base salary only. "Additional pay" on Glassdoor includes bonus and sometimes stock.
- All synthesized ladders [EST] triangulate several inputs: posted ranges, LCAs, self-reported data, recruiter guides, proxy-statement disclosures of executive STI and LTI design, and typical grade structures. They are directional, roughly ±20%.
- Keyence's yuho average covers all employees at an average age of 34.8. It is not a strategy-specific figure.

## 9. Gaps and caveats (flagged)

- **No employer-specific strategy or corporate-development H-1B LCAs were found** for Rockwell, Siemens Corp., ABB Inc., Schneider Electric USA, Emerson, Honeywell, Mitsubishi Electric US, Keyence CoA or Omron. These firms' sponsorship is dominated by engineering roles. Proxy data from the general title pool (2024) was used instead.
- Glassdoor and Levels.fyi company pages for Honeywell, Rockwell and Emerson corp dev were empty or locked. Schneider's Levels.fyi corp-dev data has fewer than 3 data points.
- Kununu (DE) and OpenWork (JP) were not reached because of the search budget. German and French figures are mostly [EST].
- The Omron and Mitsubishi Electric yuho averages were not directly verified. They are labeled [EST] and should be confirmed from EDINET.
- Data for China and Inovance is thin. Liepin postings are monthly ranges with 13–16 pay months.
- No Rockwell or Emerson strategy-titled posting with a pay range was found. Rockwell's sales-director posting is shown only as a pay-mix reference.
- The Siemens Livonia MI posting likely belongs to Siemens Digital Industries Software or another US unit. The Siemens Corp. legal entity was not confirmed.
- FX conversions are approximate.
