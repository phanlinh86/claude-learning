As of 28 October 2026. Reader: senior corporate-strategy professional (15 yrs) evaluating long-term employers in factory automation.

## Executive summary

1. **Industrial software is consolidating into the big automation strategics. Few large independents remain.**
   - AVEVA belongs to Schneider (taken private in 2023).
   - AspenTech belongs to Emerson (minority buy-out completed in 2025).
   - Altair and Dotmatics went to Siemens (2025).
   - Hexagon's Design & Engineering unit went to Cadence (€2.7B; announced Sep 2025, closed 2026).
   - Kepware and ThingWorx went from PTC to TPG (~$725M; closed 13 Mar 2026).
   - Hexagon spun off Octave (Nasdaq: OCTV) in May 2026.
   - Only Dassault, PTC, Octave and Autodesk remain as sizeable listed pure-plays. Strategy and corporate-development (corp dev) roles there are about portfolio pruning plus AI and agentic platform bets, not empire-building.
2. **Growth is mid-single-digit to high-single-digit, with very high margins.**
   - PTC: ARR +9% at constant currency (cc), non-GAAP operating margin ~41%.
   - Dassault: revenue +4% cc, non-IFRS margin ~32%.
   - AVEVA: ARR +10%.
   - Octave: ARR +7%, but total revenue flat to slightly down, plus a $2.1B impairment in Q2 2026. That is a warning sign for the spin-off.
3. **Machine vision is recovering; robot OEMs are restructuring.**
   - Cognex is growing again: Q2 2026 +17%, FY2026 guidance $1.13–1.15B, and adjusted operating margin back above 30%.
   - Teradyne Robotics cut ~10% of staff in Jan 2025 and ~14% in Nov 2025, then grew 33% in Q2 2026.
   - KUKA is cutting 560 jobs in Augsburg.
   - Zebra exited its robotics business (Fetch) in Q4 2025.
4. **Deal-making is hot, and the money and roles are in physical AI and in power/data-center equipment.**
   - Industrial automation M&A hit a record 207 deals in 2025.
   - Industrial software deals were priced at ~21.7x EV/EBITDA.
   - Robotics venture funding reached **$18.8B in 2026 YTD as of June**, already above 2025's full-year $15.0B record.
   - Data-center electrical vendors are posting triple-digit order growth (Vertiv +252% organic orders in Q4 2025; Eaton data-center orders +200%).
5. **Fastest-growing adjacent fields for a senior strategist, in order:**
   1. Data-center power and thermal (Vertiv, Eaton, Schneider, Hubbell)
   2. Physical AI and robotics startups and their corporate-venture-capital (CVC) arms
   3. PE operating and portfolio roles in industrial tech (sponsors now net sellers, so exit-prep work)
   4. Management consulting industrial practices, which is the weakest: the mid-level is being thinned.

### PTC

**Revenue.** Q3 FY26 (quarter to 30 Jun 2026) revenue was $600M, down 7% as reported. The decline is mostly a divestiture and timing effect under ASC 606 (the US revenue-recognition standard). FCF was $249M, up 3% [R].

**Strategy.**
- CEO Neil Barua has narrowed PTC to the "Intelligent Product Lifecycle": Creo (CAD), Windchill (PLM), Codebeamer (ALM) and Servigistics (service parts).
- PTC sold Kepware and ThingWorx (its industrial-connectivity and IoT businesses) to **TPG for up to ~$725M** (announced Nov 2025 [R, Capstone]; closed **13 Mar 2026**).
- The sale produced a $462.6M gain and $523M of cash proceeds [R].
- The release mentions a "go-to-market realignment" but discloses no restructuring charge [R].

**Layoffs.** None disclosed in FY26 filings. PTC is typically rumoured to cut modestly each year [EST].

**Career read.** A focused, high-margin product-lifecycle (PLM/ALM) company. Strategy work is centred on AI and agentic product development and on capital return. There is limited factory-floor scope now that the IoT assets are gone.

### Dassault Systèmes

**Strategy.**
- CEO Pascal Daloz is repositioning 3DEXPERIENCE as an "agent-based platform" [R].
- Dassault bought ArisGlobal, a life-sciences safety and regulatory software company, to rebuild Life Sciences after Medidata softened [R].
- DELMIA, its manufacturing operations and simulation brand, is not broken out in the Q2 2026 release.

**Growth.** 2026 is a reset year at ~+4% cc. The IFRS operating margin jumped to 23% from 15.9% [R].

**Layoffs.** None disclosed [R].

**Career read.** Stable, French and family-anchored. Strategy roles sit in Vélizy and Waltham and lean towards long-horizon platform themes.

### Hexagon and Octave

**Hexagon portfolio moves.** Hexagon has been pruning hard:
- Design & Engineering (simulation and CAE software) went to **Cadence for €2.7B**: 70% cash, 30% Cadence shares. D&E had ~€265M of revenue at above-group margin. Completed in 2026 [R].
- A cost programme targets **€74M in savings by Q4 2026**, with a €51M run rate already reached [R].
- Headcount fell from 17,778 to 16,118 year on year [R].
- CEO: Anders Svensson.

**Hexagon RemainCo** (the business left after the spin-off) covers:
- Manufacturing Intelligence (metrology plus production software)
- Geosystems
- Autonomous solutions
- Robotics (AEON humanoid) [EST]

**Octave.**
- Contains the former Asset Lifecycle Intelligence, Safety & Infrastructure, and Geospatial businesses: about 7,200 people across 45 countries; CEO Mattias Stenberg [R].
- Listed on **Nasdaq (OCTV)** with Swedish depositary receipts (SDRs) in Stockholm, separated in May 2026 [R].
- In Q2 2026 it booked a **$2.1B impairment** while licences fell 23% and SaaS grew 21% [R].
- Net debt is about $340M [D].

**Career read.** Octave is a newly independent company, so expect a build-out of its own strategy and corp-dev function, but with balance-sheet and growth pressure. It is a plausible take-private target [EST].

### AVEVA (Schneider)

- AVEVA ARR grew **+10%** at Q3 2026; North America and Europe grew strong double digits [R].
- Schneider group Q3 2026 revenue was €11.46B, +16.9% organic. Industrial Automation was +8.1% organic, with discrete automation up double digits and Process & Hybrid down [R].
- Schneider's FY26 plan includes ~€450M of restructuring costs [R].
- Schneider bought **Cognite** (industrial DataOps software) for **$3.1B (Jun 2026)** at ~18x revenue [R, Capstone].

### AspenTech (Emerson)

- Emerson Q3 FY26 sales were $4.87B (+6% underlying). The Software & Systems segment was +11% at a 31.8% adjusted EBITA margin [R].
- Restructuring costs were $99M in the quarter versus $41M a year earlier, concentrated in Final Control and Test & Measurement [R].
- AspenTech ARR was not disclosed in the release. **Gap.**
