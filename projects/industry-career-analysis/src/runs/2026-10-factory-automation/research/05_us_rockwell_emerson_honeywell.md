- **Core:** the Logix PLC/PAC family (ControlLogix, CompactLogix) and Studio 5000. This is the dominant controller installed base in North America [M].

### 1e. Downturn resilience
- **Weakness:** high operating leverage and exposure to OEM/discrete short cycles. FY24 showed sales -9%, adjusted EPS about -20%, and FCF roughly halved [D/M].
- **Strengths:** Rockwell stayed solidly profitable (segment margin was never below about 19%). Its balance sheet is moderate, it kept a dividend-aristocrat record (no cut), and its installed-base lock-in is strong.
- **Resilience score [EST]:** medium. Earnings are cyclical, but the franchise is durable. In a downturn, cuts land first on SG&A and contractors, while R&D is protected.

## 6. Downturn scenario (whole-market -10% revenue shock) [EST]
- **Rockwell:** sales go from about $9B to $8.1B. With a decremental margin of about 35%, segment operating income falls about 15–18% to roughly 18% margin, as in FY24. Expect another 3–5% headcount action and a dividend held.
- **Emerson:** sales fall about 8% (the process backlog cushions it). EBITA margin holds at 25%+, and FCF stays above $3B. Buybacks would flex down. Expect restructuring of $200–300M.
- **Honeywell Technologies:** sales fall about 6–8%, with Building and PA&T services as ballast. IA would see about -15%. Expect repositioning of $300M+.
- **All three survive comfortably.** Investment-grade balance sheets, FCF and dividends are maintained. The risk is to jobs, not solvency.
