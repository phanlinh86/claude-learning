How to Play

Click or tap two shells to flip them (or use the ←↑→↓ keys and Space). Find the matching sea friends!

Every match pops a cupcake for Foxy and refills a little air. Match in a row for a combo!

Watch Foxy's air meter. Clear the reef before it runs out!

Each season the water pulls a different way: shells float up, stay put, drift sideways or sink. Keep your eye on them!

Other keys: Esc/P pause · M sound · F11 full screen
