## Name and pitch
**Clover's Farm Hop.** Clover the little frog bounces up and up from the farmyard — off hay bales, fence planks, rolling hay carts and puffy clouds — catching fireflies for her lantern, while the wind keeps changing its mind!

## Core loop
Clover bounces automatically every time she lands on something. The player only steers left and right. The camera follows her up; the higher she climbs, the more points. Fireflies float between the platforms: catch them for points and to fill the lantern (a full lantern = one heart back).

## Controls
- Keyboard: hold ← / → (or A / D). Esc / P pause, M sound, F11 full screen.
- Mouse / touch: hold the left or right half of the screen.

## Score and lose
- +1 point per new metre climbed, +10 per firefly, a bonus for every new weather level.
- Missing every platform and falling off the bottom of the screen costs a heart; a bouncy trampoline catches Clover and throws her back up. No hearts left → game over. Easy has 5 hearts, Normal and Hard have 3.

## Levels (weather) — distinct backgrounds and ambient animation
1. **Sunny Meadow** – blue sky, petals and butterflies, hay bales and planks only.
2. **Rainy Orchard** – grey-green sky, falling rain and splashes, moving hay carts and puff clouds appear.
3. **Windy Hill** – golden sky, kites, leaves streaming in the wind; the gusts are strongest here.
4. **Snowy Rooftops** – pink-lilac winter dusk, falling snow in depth layers, snow caps on the platforms.
After the snow the weather loops back to sunshine, a little harder each time.

## The twist
**Wind gusts that change direction.** Every few seconds a weather vane in the HUD warns which way the next gust blows; then the wind pushes Clover sideways for a few seconds, together with the leaves, rain, snow and fireflies. Each gust can blow the other way.

## Customisation
The player can swap the pictures of the hero, the firefly and the four weather backgrounds (a pet, a family photo or a drawing).

## Audience and platform
Family-friendly, for young children (nothing scary; falling is a soft "boing" onto a trampoline). Browser (desktop, iPhone, iPad) + a portable Windows exe.
