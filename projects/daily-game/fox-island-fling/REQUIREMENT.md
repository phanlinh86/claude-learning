## Name and pitch
**Finn's Island Fling** — Finn the little pirate fox flings himself from a palm-tree slingshot across the sea, snatching magic leaves in mid-air and landing on the treasure raft. As the day turns to night only the lanterns and glowing leaves light the way.

## Core loop
1. Finn sits in the slingshot on the beach. Drag back (anywhere on the screen) to stretch the band; dotted arc shows where he will fly.
2. Let go: Finn flies in a real ballistic arc. Leaves he touches are collected. One tap in the air = a tail-flutter (a small lift, once per flight).
3. Land on the floating raft (bullseye on the X for a bonus) or splash softly into the sea (lose a heart). Finn pops back into the slingshot. New raft position and new leaves every shot.

## Controls
- Touch / mouse: drag back and release; tap during flight to flutter.
- Keyboard: ←/→ power, ↑/↓ angle, Space fire (Space again in flight = flutter). Esc/P pause, M sound, F11 full screen.

## Scoring and losing
- Leaf +1 (glowing moon leaf at night +2). Landing +2, bullseye +3 more, landing streak bonus +1 per streak step (max +4).
- A splash costs a heart (Easy 5, Normal 3, Hard 3). 0 hearts → game over. Every 20 leaves gives a heart back.

## Levels (5 landings each, then loop slightly harder)
1. Sunny Cove — big still raft, long aiming arc.
2. Jungle Isle — raft drifts gently.
3. Coral Lagoon — smaller drifting raft, a friendly seagull floats across (Finn bonks off it).
4. Lantern Bay — small raft, two gulls, shorter aiming arc.

## Twist: day → night
One day lasts about 2½ minutes (dawn, day, dusk, night). At night the screen gets dark: you only see around Finn, the raft's lantern, the slingshot torch and the glowing leaves, and the aiming arc gets shorter. Night leaves glow and are worth 2.

## Audience and platform
Family-friendly (ages 4+), nothing scary: splashes are a soft "bloop". Browser (iPhone / iPad / desktop, GitHub Pages) + portable Windows exe.
