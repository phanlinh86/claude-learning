How to Play

Drag back anywhere on the screenDrag back with the mouse and let go to fling Finn. The dots show where he will fly.

Catch the magic leaves in the air. 20 leaves give you a heart. Glowing moon leaves at night are worth 2!

Land on the raft for points, on the red X for a bullseye! Splashing into the sea costs a heart.

Tap once while flying to flutter a little higher. Seagulls bonk you away. At night only the lanterns light the way!
