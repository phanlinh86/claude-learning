How to Play

Hold Space (or ↓)Hold your finger on the screen to let the hook sink. Let go and it floats up. Touch a gem to hook it!

Reel it in: press Spacetap when the needle is in the green. The gold middle is a Perfect pull! Three misses and the gem wriggles away.

Purple gems = 10, green (deeper) = 20, red (deepest) = 30. The rare golden gem = 50!

Don't bump a pufferfish, sinking or floating up: poof! the line snaps. You have 3 lines; clear a zone to get one back.

Other keys: Esc/P pause · M sound · F11 full screen
