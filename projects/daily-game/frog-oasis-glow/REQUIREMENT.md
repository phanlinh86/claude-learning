## Pitch
Pip the little frog lives by a desert oasis, and tonight the oasis needs light! Desert friends (a fennec kit, a jerboa and a tortoise) peek out of their hidey-holes holding glowing fireflies. Tap a friend and Pip leaps over for a gentle head-boop: the friend giggles and the firefly zooms into Pip's lantern jar. But some friends are napping. Shh, don't wake them! A rare **golden firefly** is worth 5.

## Core loop
Spot a friend holding a firefly → tap before they duck → Pip hops in an arc and boops their head → the firefly flies into the jar → points, combo grows. Leave sleeping friends alone.

## Controls
- **Touch / mouse:** tap or click a friend (the whole hidey-hole counts).
- **Keyboard:** number keys 1–9 (back row first, left → right); Esc/P pause; M sound.

## Score and lose
- Firefly: +10 × multiplier (×2 from combo 5, ×3 from combo 10). Golden firefly: +50 × multiplier. Chapter finished: +50.
- Booping a sleeping friend: they wake up surprised, Pip gets dizzy, the combo resets, lose a lily-pad heart.
- A friend with a firefly ducks un-booped: lose a heart (not on Easy).
- Start with 4 hearts (max 5); +1 heart every 12 fireflies. 0 hearts = the story ends (game over).

## Levels: four storybook chapters
Each chapter opens with a storybook banner ("Chapter 2 · The Palm Grove") and a page-turn transition.
1. **The Morning Spring:** reed clumps by the water, dragonflies, 6 holes.
2. **The Palm Grove:** woven baskets under date palms, butterflies, 8 holes.
3. **The Sunset Dunes:** sand burrows between rocks, sand wisps and a rolling tumbleweed, 9 holes, faster.
4. **The Starlit Oasis:** clay jars by the moonlit pool, ambient fireflies and twinkling stars, 9 holes.
15 fireflies finish a chapter. After chapter 4 the book starts again, a bit faster.

## Customisation
The player can swap the pictures of Pip, the friends (one shared picture) and the four chapter backgrounds.

## Audience and platform
Family-friendly (ages 4+), nothing scary, original art and sound. Browser (iPhone / iPad / desktop, portrait and landscape) and a portable Windows exe.
