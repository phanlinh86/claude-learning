How to Play

Coins roll along the toy tracks. Tap when a coin reaches the ring, right on the beat, and Kit catches it!

Upper track: ↑ W F · lower track: ↓ S J.Tap the top half of the screen for the upper track, the bottom half for the lower one. On Easy any tap works!

Catches fill Kit's groove meter; misses drain it. Keep catching for a combo: ×2, ×3, ×4 points!

Every 30 s Baron Fluff lobs soft pillows. Tap them when they reach the ring to bop them back!

Other keys: Esc/P pause · M sound · F11 full screen
