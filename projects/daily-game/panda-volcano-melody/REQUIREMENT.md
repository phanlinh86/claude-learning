## Pitch
The little volcano on Tofu's island doesn't spit lava — it hums, and puffs out **musical notes**! Walk Tofu the panda around the island to scoop them up; every note you catch plays the next note of the island's song. Grab notes quickly one after another to build a combo — at combo 5, **Coco the parrot** flies in to help for a while. Watch out for the grumpy-but-harmless pebble crabs that scuttle after you: a pinch makes Tofu dizzy and costs a heart. Finish the song to move on to the next season.

## Core loop
Notes pop up on the meadow and burst out of the volcano on arcs (with a shadow, so you can see where they will land). Steer Tofu to them. Each note fills the season's song meter; a full meter finishes the season ("Spring song done!"), then the island changes season and the song gets longer. The crabs slowly follow Tofu and can't climb over the volcano, rocks or palms, so you can dodge around them.

## Controls
- **Touch / mouse:** press and hold anywhere — Tofu walks towards your finger. Let go to stop.
- **Keyboard:** arrows / WASD (8 directions). Esc/P pause, M sound, F11 full screen.

## Score and lose
- A note is worth 10, +2 for every step of the current combo (up to +20).
- Combo: catch the next note within ~2.4 s of the previous one. At combo 5, Coco the parrot arrives for 10 s, picks up notes near Tofu (they count for you) and shoos the crabs away.
- Season clear bonus: +50, +50 more if no heart was lost.
- Tofu has 3 hearts (5 on Easy). A crab pinch costs one, then Tofu is safe for 2 s. A rare mango restores a heart.
- No hearts left → Tofu sits down for a nap → game over.

## Levels
Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter, each with its own meadow, props (palms, rocks, bushes) and ambient animation (blossom petals, butterflies and sun motes, tumbling leaves, snow in depth layers), and volcano smoke puffs. More crabs and a longer song each season. After winter the year starts again, a little faster.

## Customisation
The player can swap Tofu, the musical note and the island meadow for their own pictures.

## Audience and platform
Family-friendly; spring on Easy must be comfortable for a 4–6-year-old. Browser (iPhone, iPad, desktop) + portable Windows exe.
