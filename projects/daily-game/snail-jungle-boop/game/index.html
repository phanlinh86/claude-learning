How to Play

Zippy's light and the big star at the top show his colour. Each colour has its own shape too: heart, sun, drop, moon.

TapClick (or press 1–9) a friend holding a star of that colour. Zippy zooms over and boops it!

A wrong colour makes Zippy dizzy and costs a leaf. Every 12 boops give a leaf back. No leaves left: game over.

Zippy changes colour every few boops (he blinks first). Fill the green ring (15 boops) to fly to the next part of the jungle!

1–9 boop a burrow (back row first) · Esc/P pause · M sound · F11 full screen
