## Pitch
Zippy the snail has a tiny jetpack on his shell and a glowing light that changes colour. Jungle friends (a monkey, a frog and a toucan) peek out of leafy burrows holding coloured stars. Tap a friend whose star matches Zippy's colour and he zooms over for a gentle *boop*: the friend giggles and the star is yours. Tap the wrong colour and Zippy gets dizzy!

## Core loop
Watch Zippy's colour → spot a friend holding a star of that colour → tap it before it ducks back down → Zippy jets over and boops → +points, combo grows. Every few boops Zippy's colour changes.

## Controls
- **Touch / mouse:** tap or click a critter (the tap zone is generous: the whole burrow counts).
- **Keyboard:** number keys 1–9 tap burrows (left→right, back row first); Esc/P pause; M sound.

## Score and lose
- Matching boop: +10 × multiplier (×1, ×2 from combo 5, ×3 from combo 10). Level cleared: +50.
- Wrong colour tap: Zippy gets dizzy for a moment, combo resets, lose a leaf (heart).
- A matching friend ducks down un-booped: lose a leaf (not on Easy).
- Start with 3 leaves (max 5); +1 leaf every 12 boops. 0 leaves = game over.
- Each star also has a shape emblem (heart, sun, drop, moon) so colour-blind players can match by shape.

## Levels: four jungle biomes
1. **Riverbank Morning:** mud burrows among reeds, dragonflies, 6 burrows, 2 colours.
2. **Bamboo Grove:** bamboo-stump pots, drifting leaves, 8 burrows, 3 colours.
3. **Treetop Canopy:** hollow branch knots high in the trees, butterflies, 9 burrows, 3 colours, faster.
4. **Waterfall Ruins at Dusk:** mossy stone pots, mist and fireflies, 9 burrows, 4 colours.

15 boops clear a biome. After the ruins the jungle loops (round 2) a bit faster.

## Customisation
The player can swap the pictures of Zippy, the critters (one shared picture), and the four biome backgrounds.

## Audience
Family-friendly, kids 4+ (Easy is the default). Nothing scary: giggles, boops and stars.

## Platform
Browser (iPhone, iPad, desktop) on GitHub Pages, plus a portable Windows exe.
