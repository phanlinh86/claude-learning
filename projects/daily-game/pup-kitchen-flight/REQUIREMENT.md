## Pitch
Biscuit is a little robot pup with a spinning propeller on his head. Grandma is making soup, and the herb leaves have floated all over the kitchen! Tap to puff Biscuit up, glide past hanging ladles, pot stacks and steamy kettles, and catch every mint, basil and bay leaf. The day passes as you fly: sunny morning, rainy noon, windy sunset and a snowy night, when the lights go down and only Biscuit's nose lamp lights the way.

## Core loop
Tap → Biscuit puffs up; let go → he glides down in a gentle arc. Steer through the gaps between hanging utensils (from the top rail) and pot stacks (on the counter), and fly through the floating leaves.

## Controls
- **Touch / mouse:** tap or click anywhere to puff.
- **Keyboard:** Space, ↑ or W to puff; Esc/P pause; M sound; F11 full screen.

## Score and lose
- Each leaf: +1. Each obstacle passed: +1. A clean level (no bumps): +5.
- Bumping into a utensil, a stack, a kettle or the counter: lose one battery, bounce back, blink safely for 1.5 s (2.5 s on Easy).
- Batteries: Easy starts with 4 and gets one back every 6 leaves; Normal 3 / every 25; Hard 3 / every 30 (tuned by the playtest). 0 batteries: Biscuit powers down and floats to the counter → game over.
- Kettles puff up a column of steam that gently lifts Biscuit (it never hurts).

## Levels (about 60 s each), with the twist
1. **Sunny Morning:** bright, sunbeams and dust motes; slow and wide gaps (easy for a 6-year-old).
2. **Rainy Noon:** rain on the windows, cooler light, kettles appear.
3. **Windy Sunset:** curtains flap; wind gusts (with a 1 s warning) gently push Biscuit up or down; orange light.
4. **Snowy Night:** snow outside, the room gets dark; Biscuit's nose lamp lights a circle ahead, the leaves glow softly and the obstacles get a soft outline.
After the night the next day starts (round 2), a little faster.

## Customisation
The player can swap the pictures of Biscuit, the leaf and the kitchen wall background.

## Audience
Family-friendly, kids 4+ (Easy is the default). Nothing scary: bonks, boings and giggles; the dark is cozy, not spooky.

## Platform
Browser (iPhone, iPad, desktop) on GitHub Pages, plus a portable Windows exe.
