How to Play

Slide your finger to move Mossy, let go to drop the eggMove the mouse to move Mossy, click to drop the egg. The dots show where it will land.

Two matching eggs that touch merge into a bigger egg! Chain merges for a combo. Two Golden Dragon eggs hatch a baby dragon (+100)!

Watch the windsock: when it flutters, a gust is coming and falling eggs blow sideways. Aim with the breeze!

Keep the eggs under the vine. If they stay above it too long the game ends — but on Easy and Normal a fairy helps first!
