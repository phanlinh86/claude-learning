How to Play

Move Puff with ← → / A D, or move the mouse / hold your finger where Puff should float.

Watch the volcano rumble, then run under the flying coins. Gold is worth 5! Fill the pouch to clear the zone.

Catch coin after coin to grow your combo: ×2, ×3… up to ×5 points! A coin that lands in the sand resets it.

Don't catch the warm sizzly rocks: that's a heart! Press Space (or 💧) for Cool Rain to turn rocks above Puff into steam.

Other keys: Esc/P pause · M sound · F11 full screen
