# Nini's Sea Painting · Rùa Nini Tô Màu Đại Dương

Rolled 2026-11-07 (America/Los_Angeles) with `roll.py 2026-11-07 4`:
mechanic **paint the picture: tap each part of a line drawing to fill it with the colour asked for** (family *trace*;
avoided: build, container, music), theme **under the sea**, helper **a baby turtle**, weather **spring → summer → autumn →
winter**. No swap: the seasons are seen from under the water (blossom petals on the surface, summer sun rays, autumn
leaves drifting down, winter ice on top with snowflakes sinking). Mầm's Treasure Reef (Oct 31) was also under the sea, but
it is 7 games ago and played a dig/sort game in lagoons; this one is a painting studio on the seabed.

## Pitch
Nini, a baby sea turtle in a cream painter's beret, paints pictures on a big shell-framed canvas on the seabed. Each
round a new line drawing floats in — a fish, a crab, a sailboat, a submarine with funny-shaped windows, a whale blowing
bubbles. Nini asks: "Paint the tail BLUE!" — the child taps the blue paint shell, then taps the shining tail, and the
colour splashes and spreads inside the lines. Shapes: "Paint the TRIANGLE window!", counting: "Paint 3 bubbles!" (counted
aloud), animals: "Paint the CRAB!". When the picture is done, Nini's brush sweeps colour over the rest and the picture
**comes alive** — the fish wiggles and swims off the canvas, the crab scuttles away, the boat sails. Above the water the
seasons turn: spring petals, summer sun rays, autumn leaves, winter ice and snow.

## What feels different from the last games
- Last games: drag cargo into train cars (container), tap a band (music), stack blocks (build). Here the gesture is
  **tap-to-paint** with a growing colour fill clipped to the drawing's lines, and the reward is the drawing coming alive.
- Layout: one big canvas in the middle, a row of paint shells under it, Nini beside it — no drop zones, no containers.

## Core loop
Picture floats in → Nini asks (voice + bubble) → colours: tap the right paint shell (the brush dips), then tap the
shining part → paint spreads → next part. Other topics: the brush is already loaded; tap the right part (shape window,
animal, letter bubble, N bubbles counted aloud). After the last step the rest of the picture is coloured with a sweep and
it comes alive (swims / scuttles / sails away) → stars → next picture. 4 rounds per chapter (warm-up: 1 step), then
Talk time (one spoken question, picture choices + mic), next chapter.

## Rules
No fail state, no timer pressure. Wrong paint or wrong part → it wobbles, kind words ("That's yellow. Let's find blue!");
2 misses → the right one glows and a ghost hand taps it. Tapping a part that isn't asked for in a colour step is not a
mistake ("Paint the shining tail!"). Stars 3 / 2 / 1 per round (0 / 1 / ≥ 2 misses), only up.
Surprises (one round per chapter, rotating, never the warm-up): **fishy** — a little striped fish swims past carrying a
paint bubble; tap it: it pops a shower of paint dots and giggles; **pearl** — a clam on the sand; tap it: it opens and a
pearl sparkles (no score effect).

## Voice (EN / VI)
Every instruction spoken + shown in a bubble; tap Nini to repeat. Colour/shape/animal names on every tap, counting aloud,
praise with the child's name. Helper: EN "Nini", VI "Rùa Nini" in titles, "Nini" in lines. Talk-time question per chapter.
