## Pitch
Mochi, a round little panda baker in a cream chef's hat, decorates cookies at her kitchen counter. "Let's pipe a RED wave!"
— the child taps the red icing bag, then follows the glowing dots on the big cookie with a finger: glossy icing squeezes out
of the bag right under the finger. Shapes: "Which cookie is the TRIANGLE?" → it slides onto the tray, then the child pipes
icing all around its edge. Counting: find the cookie with number 3, trace the 3, then tap 3 chocolate chips onto it, counted
aloud. Animals: "Find the DOG cookie — woof!" → pipe an icing road from the dog to its kennel and the dog cookie hops along
the road home. Every finished cookie sparkles, hops off the tray and lands on the cooling rack. Through the kitchen window
the seasons turn: spring blossoms, summer sun, autumn leaves, winter snow.

## What feels different from the last games
- Gesture: **trace** (press and move the finger along a path). Taps are only used for choices.
- Reward: the icing gleams, sprinkles rain, the cookie hops onto a growing cooling-rack row (4 per chapter).
- Layout: one big baking tray in the middle, a row of icing bags / choice cookies on the counter, Mochi beside it.

## Learning goals by age (4 chapters, easiest first, one per season)
| age | chapters |
|---|---|
| 3 | colours (2 bags) · shapes (2) · big & small · animals → homes (2) |
| 4 (default) | colours (3 bags) · shapes (3) · counting 1–5 (find the numeral, trace it, place N chips) · animals → homes (3) |
| 5 | shapes (4, mixed icing colours) · counting 1–10 · capital letters (find + trace) · animals → homes (4) |
| 6 | capital ↔ small letters · first letter of a word · adding within 10 · counting to 12 |
| 7+ | adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · first letter · odd & even |
Tracing a shape / numeral / letter is the learning "write it" step; the choice before it is the "know it" step.

## Core loop
Cookie / tray slides in → Mochi asks (voice + bubble) → choice: tap the right icing bag or the right cookie (a mistake if
wrong) → trace: follow the dots from the glowing start; icing appears only where the finger follows the path; lifting the
finger keeps the progress → (count: tap N chips) → finish sparkle + sprinkles → the cookie hops to the cooling rack →
stars → next round. 4 rounds per chapter (warm-up: easiest, fewest choices), then Talk time, next chapter.

## Rules
No fail state, no timer. Tracing is never "wrong": going off the path just stops the icing, Mochi says "Follow the dots!";
after a while a ghost hand shows the way, and a struggling child gets "magic icing" (the path fills as the finger moves
anywhere on the cookie). Wrong choice → it wobbles, kind words ("That's the circle. Where is the triangle?"); 2 misses → the
right one glows and a ghost hand taps it. Stars 3 / 2 / 1 per round by choice mistakes (0 / 1 / ≥ 2), only up.
Surprises (one round per chapter, rotating, never the warm-up): **bird** — a little bird flutters past the window side
of the kitchen; tap: it chirps and drops a sprinkle shower; **mouse** — a mouse peeks out of the flour sack; tap: a puff of
flour and a giggle (no score effect).

## Voice (EN / VI)
Every instruction spoken + shown in the bubble; tap Mochi to repeat. Names on every choice, counting aloud, praise with the
child's name. Helper: EN "Mochi", VI "Gấu Trúc Mochi" in titles, "Mochi" in lines (TTS VI "Mô Chi"). One Talk-time
question per chapter (picture choices + mic).
