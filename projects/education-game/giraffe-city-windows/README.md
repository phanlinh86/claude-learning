# Gigi's Peekaboo Windows · Hươu Gigi Mở Cửa Sổ

A memory learning game for children aged 3–7 (default 4). Gigi, a giraffe calf with a mint scarf, lives on Peekaboo Street,
where every apartment building has windows with little wooden shutters — and something hides behind each pair. The
windows open for a quick peek and close again; then the child taps a window: the shutters swing open on their hinges,
Gigi stretches her long neck to look and says what is inside. Find the other window with the same thing and both windows
light up and their flower boxes bloom. Open every pair and the whole building cheers with confetti, then Gigi trots down
the street to the next one. The weather changes per chapter: sunny → breezy (kites, flapping awnings) → rain (cosy lit
windows) → rainbow.

Made on 2026-11-18 with `/build-education-game random 4` (see `REQUIREMENT.md` for the roll and the one swap: jigsaw →
memory, because a jigsaw on a city street was Pom's Puzzle Town nine days earlier).

**Play:** https://phanlinh86.github.io/claude-learning/projects/education-game/giraffe-city-windows/game/

## Ages and topics (4 chapters per session, one weather each; topics rotate between sessions)
| age | chapters (find the pairs) |
|---|---|
| 3 | colours · shapes · big & small · animals |
| 4 (default) | colours (same thing, same colour) · shapes · counting 1–5 (numeral ↔ dots) · **animal homes** (dog ↔ kennel, bird ↔ nest, bee ↔ hive …) |
| 5 | shapes in mixed colours · counting 1–10 · capital letters · animal homes |
| 6 | capital ↔ small letters · first letter of a word · adding within 10 · counting to 12 |
| 7+ | first letter · adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · odd & even |

## How to play
- Tap a window to open it, then tap another. Same thing → a pair (lights + flowers). Different → Gigi says kindly what they
  were and the shutters close. In a hurry? Tapping a third window closes the two at once.
- No losing, no timer. After two misses in a row one pair glows and peeks open a crack. Tap Gigi or the bubble to hear the
  question again. Stars: 3 / 2 / 1 per building, +1 for Talk time.
- Surprises (one building per chapter, never a mistake): a **pigeon** lands on a sill, the little **bus** stops with its
  passengers, a **balloon** floats up past the windows — tap them!
- After 4 buildings: **Talk time** — one question with 2–3 picture answers (tap, or say it if the mic is on).

## On iPhone / iPad
1. Open the link in Safari and tap once anywhere (iOS only allows sound after the first tap).
2. Sound is loud by default and plays through the media volume; adjust it in settings (hold the gear 2 s) or with − / + in
   the pause menu.
3. Optional: **Share → Add to Home Screen** for a full-screen icon.
4. Vietnamese voice: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Vietnamese → download "Linh".
