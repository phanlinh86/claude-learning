# Koko's Cake Tower · Tháp Bánh Kem Của Koala Koko (education game, 2026-11-14)

Made unattended with `/build-education-game random 4` (seed 2026-11-14, America/Los_Angeles). The roller skipped the
families of the last 3 games (trace+pop, seek+container, music) and rationed *container*. Rolled: mechanic **build a
tower: stack the right blocks; the tower wobbles with real physics** (family **build**), theme **jungle**, helper
**a koala**, weather **sunny → breezy → rain → rainbow**, skills for age 4 **colours · shapes · counting 1–5 · animals**.

**One swap (sanity check): theme jungle → candy village.** The roll (tower + jungle) is the same game as Lulu's Jungle
Tower (2026-11-04), and koalas don't live in jungles. A koala baker stacking a giant layer cake in a candy village keeps
the rolled mechanic, helper and weather, but looks, sounds and ends completely differently. Candy village has never been
used.

## Pitch
Koko, a little koala baker in a cream chef's hat and a mint apron, bakes the tallest cake in Candy Village. A cherry
floats above the cake stand on a little cotton-candy cloud: "Let's build a cake all the way up to the cherry!" Three soft
cake layers wait on the counter. Koko asks "Stack the RED layer!" and the child
drags the layer up over the cake and lets go: it drops, lands with a soft *flump* and a squishy squash, and the whole
cake jiggles and settles like real sponge cake. A wrong layer boings off the frosting and hops home ("Boing! That's the
blue layer. Let's find red!"). When the cake reaches the cherry, the cherry plops on top, a candle pops up on every layer
and lights as Koko counts "one, two, three!", Koko takes a big breath and blows — *fffff* — the candles go out in little
smoke puffs and sprinkles rain down. The weather changes each chapter: sunny → breezy (the cake sways in the wind and Koko
holds it steady) → a sugar-drop rain shower → a rainbow over Candy Village.

## How it differs from the last games
No tapping bubbles (Nov 13), no odd-one-out parade (Nov 12), no chimes (Nov 10). The gesture is **drag UP and let go
to stack**; the layout is **a wide cake growing upwards in the middle**; the reward is **squishy jiggle physics, candles
that light one by one, and blowing them out**. Versus Lulu's Jungle Tower (same family, 10 days ago): wide soft cake
layers instead of wooden cubes (squishier landing, jelly jiggle), a weather cycle that physically affects the cake
(breeze sway, rain), a candle-and-blow finale instead of a monkey swing, and a honey-bee surprise.

## Audience and learning goals
- Default age 4 (3–5); the parent picks 3, 4, 5, 6 or 7+ in settings.
- Topics by age (4 chapters per session, one per weather, easiest first; chapters rotate between sessions):
  - 3: colours (2 layers to choose from) · shapes (2) · big & small layers · animal cookies (2)
  - 4: colours (3) · shapes (3, different colours) · counting 1–5 (candy dots; "count up" rounds stack 1, 2, 3 in order) · animal cookies (3, "Who says moo?" from round 3)
  - 5: shapes in mixed colours · counting 1–10 · capital letters · animals
  - 6: capital ↔ small letters · first letter of a word · adding within 10 · counting to 12
  - 7+: adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · first letter · odd & even
- A round = one cake: N layers to stack (age 4: 2, 3, 4, 4; warm-up round: 2 layers, 2 choices), each a question.
- No losing, no timers. A wrong layer bounces off and hops home; Koko says kindly what it was and repeats the goal.
  After 2 misses the right layer glows and a ghost hand shows the drag. Stars only go up: 3 / 2 / 1 per cake; +1 Talk time.

## Core loop
Layers drop onto the counter → Koko asks → child drags a layer up and lets go over the cake → it falls and lands (the
cake jiggles) → Koko names it and counts the cake → new choices drop in → … → cake reaches the cherry: cherry plops on
top, candles light while counting, Koko blows them out, sprinkles, stars → the layers hop away as slices → next cake.
4 cakes per chapter, Talk time, then the weather changes. After 4 chapters: a rainbow party with sprinkles and "Play
again". Surprise per chapter: a breeze makes the cake sway and Koko steadies it with both paws, or a honey bee lands on
the cake (tap it: it buzzes away in a loop).

## Voice
Every instruction spoken (en-US or vi-VN) and shown in a speech bubble; tap Koko or the bubble to hear it again. Koko
names a layer when it is picked up, counts the cake aloud, praises with the child's name, never negative words.
Talk-time question per chapter (picture choices for ages ≤ 5) with an optional mic answer.

## Sound

sprinkle twinkles, count chimes, bee buzz, wind whoosh, rain patter, music per weather.
