`odd one out: a row of things, tap the one that does not belong` (family **seek**) · **fairy forest** · **a little bear** ·
**sunny → breezy → rain → rainbow**. Recent games: Pom's Puzzle Town [build+container], Kiki's Camp Chimes [music],

## Pitch
Moka, a little honey-brown bear, holds a tea party for the fairies in a glowing forest clearing. Things float down on
dandelion fluff and land on leaf placemats along a mossy log table: teacups, cookies, berry plates, place cards and forest
guests. One of them does not belong! Tap it — a tiny fairy swoops in, sprinkles fairy dust, and the odd one floats up on a
sparkle trail into the fairy-light garland, where it lights a flower lantern. The matching friends hop one after another and
ring a chime tune (counting along), Moka raises his cup: "Cheers!" — and a puff of fairy dust clears the table for the next round.
Four chapters: sunny morning → breezy (leaves blow, napkins flutter) → gentle rain (Moka under a mushroom umbrella, drops
plink in the cups) → rainbow. Every chapter fills one garland of 4 lanterns.

## Core loop
1. **Float in** — 3–5 things drift down on dandelion fluff (sway, then land with a squash on their leaf placemat).
2. **Ask** — Moka asks ("They're all red… but one! Which one is NOT red?"), bubble shows the same words.
3. **Tap** the odd one → fairy swoops, dust sparkles, the item rises in a swaying float to the garland and lights the next
   lantern (lantern shows the item's colour/glow). Wrong tap: the item does a little "no-no" wiggle, Moka says kindly what it
   is and that it matches its friends, then repeats the goal. After 2 misses the odd one glows + a ghost hand shows it.
4. **Chime wave** — the matching friends hop left → right, each rings a rising bell note (counted aloud in the count chapter:
   the odd plate's berries are counted as it floats up).
5. **Cheers** — Moka raises his cup, sparkles; fairy-dust puff clears the table. Next round.
Round ends when the odd one is found. 4 rounds per chapter, Talk time after each chapter, celebration after 4 chapters.

## Stars
3 per round with no mistakes, 2 with one, 1 otherwise; an assisted round ≤ 2. Stars only go up.

## Voice lines (EN / VI, Moka)
Ask, right ("The blue cup! It's not red."), wrong ("That cup is red, like its friends. Find the one that's not red!"),
hint, idle nudge, praise with the child's name, cheers ("Cheers!" / "Cụng ly nào!"), weather greetings per chapter,
surprises (a ladybug walks along the table, a butterfly lands on the teapot, a snail under a mushroom in the rain,
fairy friends dance at the rainbow), Talk time.

## Talk time (one per chapter)
colours: show a cup, "What colour is this?" (colour blobs); shapes: "Which one is the star cookie?" (cookies);
count: show a plate, "How many berries?" (numerals with dots); animals: show a guest, "Where does the penguin live?"
(home badges; age 3: "Who is this?"). Big picture choices + mic button; tap always works.
