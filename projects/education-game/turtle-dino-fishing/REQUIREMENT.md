# Tibo's Dino Lake Fishing · Rùa Tibo Câu Cá Hồ Khủng Long

Rolled 2026-11-17 (seed = date in America/Los_Angeles, age 4):
`fishing: tap the fish that carries the answer as it swims by` (family **pop**) · **dinosaur valley** · **a baby turtle** ·
**morning → noon → sunset → starry night**. Recent games: Kiki's Camp Chimes [music], Bo's Farm Parade [seek+container],
Bao's Bubble Sky [trace+pop], Koko's Cake Tower [build], Nim's River Hop [path+container], Moka's Fairy Tea Party
[music+seek+container]. No element swapped (a baby turtle fishing in a dinosaur-valley lake fits).
Why it feels different from the last game (tea table, odd-one-out, things fall and sit still): here the targets **move** —
fish swim back and forth in lanes underwater, the child taps the one that is asked for, and a real fishing line flies out,
hooks it and reels it up on a swinging line; then the fish is set free with a big leap and a water lily blooms. Nothing is
dropped into anything (catch and release; no container).

## Pitch
Tibo, a baby turtle in a little straw sun hat, sits on a mossy rock at the edge of a sparkling lake in Dinosaur Valley,
holding a bamboo fishing rod. Friendly fish swim by — red, blue and yellow fish, fish with a star or a heart on their side,
fish with spots, and bubbles with animal friends inside. Tibo asks: "Catch the RED fish!" Tap it — swish! The line flies in
an arc, plop, the hook sinks, the fish nibbles, the rod bends, and Tibo reels it up out of the water where it wiggles and
swings on the line. "A red fish!" Then Tibo lets it go: the fish leaps high, splashes back home, and a water lily blooms on
the lake. The other fish jump for joy one after another. Four lilies fill each chapter: morning (mist, a baby pterosaur
glides by), noon (a baby long-neck dinosaur wades in and sprays water), sunset (a frog on a lily pad), starry night
(fireflies, the fish glow softly).

## Core loop
1. **Swim in** — 2–5 fish (or animal bubbles) swim in from the sides into lanes and swim back and forth with a wagging tail
   and a gentle bob, turning around at the lake ends.
2. **Ask** — Tibo asks ("Catch the RED fish!"), the bubble shows the same words; tap Tibo to hear it again.
3. **Tap** the right fish → cast (line arc, plop, ripple) → bite → reel (rod bends, the fish wriggles up, splashes out,
   swings like a pendulum on the line) → "A red fish!" (spots counted aloud: "1, 2, 3 spots!") → release leap → a lily
   blooms. Wrong tap: that fish does a little startled dart and bubbles, Tibo says kindly what it is, then repeats the goal.
   After 2 misses the right fish slows down and glows + a ghost hand shows it.
4. **Joy jumps** — the other fish leap one after another with rising notes, then swim away; new fish swim in.
Round ends when the right fish is caught. 4 rounds per chapter, Talk time after each chapter, a celebration after 4.

## Stars
3 per round with no mistakes, 2 with one, 1 otherwise; an assisted round ≤ 2. Stars only go up.

## Voice lines (EN / VI, Tibo)
Ask, right ("A red fish! Well done!"), wrong ("That one is a blue fish. Let's find the red fish!"), hint, idle nudge,
praise with the child's name, release ("Bye bye, little fish!"), weather greetings per chapter, surprises, Talk time.

## Talk time (one per chapter)
colours: show a fish, "What colour is this fish?" (paint blobs); shapes: "Which fish has the star?" (pictures);
count: show a spotted fish, "How many spots?" (numerals with dots); animals: show a friend, "Where does the penguin live?"
(home badges; age 3: "Who is this?"). Big picture choices + mic; tap always works.
