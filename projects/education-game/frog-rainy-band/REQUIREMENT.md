# Bop's Rainy Band · Ban Nhạc Mưa Của Ếch Bốp (education game, 2026-11-05)

Made unattended with `/build-education-game random 4` (seed 2026-11-05, America/Los_Angeles). The roller skipped the
families of the last 3 games (build, seek+pop, trace+path) and rationed *container*. Rolled: mechanic **animal band: tap
the drum as many times as the number / when the right animal sings** (family **music**), theme **rainy-day garden**,
helper **a giraffe calf**, weather **spring → summer → autumn → winter**, skills for age 4 **colours · shapes · counting
1–5 · animals**.
**Swap (one element):** helper giraffe calf → **a little frog**. A giraffe calf was yesterday's helper (Lulu), and a frog

## Pitch
Bop, a little aqua frog in a straw hat, leads a band in a rainy garden. Raindrops patter on the leaves, the band sits on
flower-pot stools on a wooden stage, and a song sheet with empty note circles hangs above them. Bop asks "Play the RED
drum!" — the child taps it: *boom!*, the drum squashes and bounces, a coloured note flies up into the song sheet. When
the sheet is full, the whole band plays the child's own song back, note by note, while Bop drums and the animals dance.
Counting rounds use Bop's big drum: "Drum three times!" (each tap counts aloud) or "Listen! boom-boom-boom. How many?".
Animal rounds: a duck, a cat, a cow… sit in the band: "Who says moo?" — tap the cow and it sings. The garden goes from
spring blossoms in soft rain to a sunny summer shower with a rainbow, autumn leaves in drizzle and a snowy winter.

## How it differs from the last games
No dragging (Nov 4 tower), no hiding places (Nov 3), no rocket (Nov 2), no cards, no popping balloons, no basket. The
gesture is **tap to play an instrument** (and tap a rhythm on the big drum). The layout is **a band on a stage under a
song sheet**, the reward is **music: the child's song is played back**, with dancing band members.

## Audience and learning goals
- Default age 4 (3–5); the parent picks 3, 4, 5, 6 or 7+ in settings.
- Topics by age (4 chapters, one per season, easiest first; chapters rotate between sessions):
  - 3: colours (2 drums) · shapes (2) · big & small drums · animals (2)
  - 4: colours (3) · shapes (3, different colours) · counting 1–5 (drum N times; listen and pick the number) · animals (3, "Who says moo?" from round 3)
  - 5: shapes in mixed colours (4) · counting 1–10 · capital letters · animals (4)
  - 6: capital ↔ small letters · first letter of a word · adding within 10 · counting to 12
  - 7+: adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · first letter · odd & even
- A round = one song: N notes (age 4: 2, 3, 4, 4; warm-up 2 notes with 2 choices), each note a question.
- No losing, no timers. A wrong drum goes "boing" and wobbles; Bop says kindly what it was and repeats the goal. After 2
  misses the right one glows and a ghost hand taps it. Stars only go up: 3 / 2 / 1 per song; +1 for Talk time.

## Core loop
Band hops onto the stools → Bop asks → child taps → right: the instrument plays, a note flies into the song sheet →
next ask → … → sheet full: the band plays the song back, confetti notes, stars → band hops off → next song. 4 songs
per chapter, Talk time, then the season changes. After 4 chapters: a winter party and "Play again". Surprise per
chapter: a snail with a tiny bell joins the band (tap it: ding!) or a sudden shower — raindrops play a pitter-patter tune
and Bop opens a leaf umbrella.

## Voice
Every instruction spoken (en-US or vi-VN) and shown in a speech bubble; tap Bop or the bubble to hear it again. Bop names
what was tapped, counts aloud, praises with the child's name, never negative words.

## Sound
Loud and clear on phones (iOS media channel, compressor + 1.6× makeup gain), volume slider + pause −/+. Each colour has
its own pitch, so a song of drums is a real little melody; synthesized animal voices; rain ambience; music per season.
