## Pitch
Miu, a little cream kitten in a straw hat, is holding a balloon party on the farm. Bunches of big shiny balloons float
up over the fence and bob in the breeze; the child taps the right one — POP! — confetti flies and Miu says the word.
Animal balloons carry a farm friend that floats down and joins the party on the grass. The day passes as you play:
pink morning, bright noon, orange sunset and a starry night with fireflies.

## Audience and learning goals
- Default age 4 (3–5 range). The parent picks the age (3, 4, 5, 6, 7+) in settings.
- Topics by age (4 chapters per session, one per time of day, easiest first):
  - 3: colours (2 colours) · big & small · shapes (2) · farm animals (2)
  - 4: colours (3) · shapes (3) · counting 1–5 · farm animals (3)
  - 5: shapes with mixed colours (4) · counting 1–10 · capital letters (3) · farm animals (4)
  - 6: capital ↔ small letters · first letter of a word · adding within 10 · counting to 12
  - 7+: first letter of a word · adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · odd & even
- Balloon versions: "Pop the RED balloons!", "Pop the STAR balloon!" (the balloons ARE stars, hearts, circles…),
  "Pop 3 balloons!" (Miu counts every pop aloud, the sign fills a dot per pop), "Pop the balloon with the PIG!"
  (cow, pig, sheep, duck, horse, goat, chick, bunny, puppy), number/letter balloons from age 5.
- No losing, no timers, balloons never fly away during play. A wrong tap makes the balloon boing and wobble with kind
  words; after 2 wrong taps the right balloon glows. Stars only go up: 3 per round without mistakes, 2 with one,
  1 otherwise; +1 for Talk time.

## Core loop
Miu says the task (voice + bubble, sign shows a picture of it) → balloons float up and bob → the child taps one →
right: it pops with confetti and a "pop!", Miu says the word (counting rounds: the number) and an animal friend drifts
down to the grass; wrong: the balloon squishes, boings aside and wobbles → round done when every right balloon is
popped (the rest float away up into the sky) → stars → next round. 4 rounds per chapter (a warm-up first), then
**Talk time** (one picture question, e.g. "Which animal says moo?", answer by tapping or by voice), then the sky
changes to the next time of day. After 4 chapters: a night party with all the friends and "Play again".

## Voice
- Every instruction spoken and shown in a speech bubble; tap Miu or the bubble to hear it again.
- Says the word on every pop, counts aloud, praises with the child's name, never negative words.
- English (en-US) or Vietnamese (vi-VN, warm "bé ơi … nhé!") with the device's voices.
- Talk time: optional microphone answer (Safari speech recognition) — tapping always works.
