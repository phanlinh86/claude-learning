**Interpretation.** Yesterday (Pip's Reef Tower) the child tapped blocks that a bubble carried up a tower. The last music
games were Bop's Rainy Band (11-05: band members on stools) and Kiki's Camp Chimes (11-10: chimes hanging from a beam that
swing like pendulums). This game is different in gesture, layout and reward:
- **Gesture: drumming.** Bouncy cloud drums STAND on little floating cloud cushions across the middle of the screen. A tap
  makes the drum **boom**: it squashes, hops up off its cushion and lands again, its skin ripples, the cushion squishes.
  Counting is **beat the big drum N times** (each beat counted aloud) — a rhythm gesture, not a choice.
- **Layout:** drums on a row of floating clouds at staggered heights, a string of little star lanterns across the sky,
  Bibi the puppy bottom-left on a cloud with two drumsticks, a cymbal on a stand next to him.
- **Reward:** each right beat sends a music note up that lights a **star lantern**; at the end of a round the child plays the
  song (tap or swipe the drums) — every drum bounces in a wave and Bibi ends it with a **cymbal crash** and a sparkle burst.

## Pitch
Bibi the puppy plays drums on the clouds of a rainbow land. "Boom the RED drum!" — tap it: BOOM, it bounces and a note
flies up to light a star. "Beat the big drum three times!" — boom, boom, boom: "One, two, three!" "Who says moo? Tap the
cow!" — the cow peeking over her drum sings "Moo!". Then play the song: the drums bounce one after another and Bibi
crashes the cymbal. The day passes from a pink morning to a starry night.

## Core loop
1. Little cloud cushions puff in; drums drop from the sky onto them, squash and bounce, settling with a gentle bob. From round 2
   one extra drum that does not belong is there too (ages ≥ 4).
2. Bibi asks (voice + bubble). The child taps a drum: BOOM. The first drum touched is the answer; other drums brushed by a swipe
   just boom softly (never a mistake).
3. Right → bright boom + sparkle, its name is said, a note flies up and lights the next star lantern. Another drum → a soft
   "bumpf", it wobbles, kind words ("That's the blue drum. Let's boom the red one!") — counts only for stars.
4. Counting (ages ≤ 4): one big drum and a number card ("3" with 3 dots). Each beat lights a dot and is counted aloud; the Nth
   beat completes it. Stopping halfway → "Two! One more!" No mistakes possible.
5. All asks done → **song**: "Now play our song — tap the drums!" Any tap or swipe starts the wave: every drum bounces in turn,
   lanterns flash, Bibi drums along and crashes the cymbal. Auto after 4 s. Drums hop up and float away; next set.
6. After 4 rounds (or ≥ 3 rounds and 75 s): **Talk time** — one question with 2–3 big picture choices + mic.
7. 4 chapters (morning, noon, sunset, night) → celebration (Bibi dances, all star lanterns glow, confetti) → Play again.

## Stars and kindness
3 per round with no mistake, 2 with one, 1 otherwise; +1 for a right Talk-time answer; stars only go up. No fail state, no
timer. After 2 misses the right drum glows and a ghost hand taps it; hint cooldown 6 s; idle nudges back off ×1.5 up to 30 s.
Tapping Bibi repeats the question. Surprises (one round per chapter, never the warm-up): a **fluffy cloud lamb** trots by
(tap → it puffs up round and bounces), a **little bird** lands on the lantern string (tap → it sings back the last note); at
night **little twinkle stars** float down to listen (tap → they sparkle and zip off).

## Talk time (per chapter)
Show one drum and ask "What colour is this drum?" / "Which one is the star?" / "How many dots?" (numerals with dots) / "Who says
moo?" with 2–3 picture choices and an optional mic.
