## Pitch
Tupi the baby elephant bakes treats for the whole cookie town. "Ding! A red cupcake is ready! Take it to the red house!" The
child holds Tupi's little cart and drives it along the chocolate-biscuit road — at the fork, which way? — to the right
gingerbread house. Knock knock: the door opens, a friend says thank you and the chimney puffs little hearts. Blossoms in the
bakery window in spring, sunbeams in summer, leaves blowing in in autumn, snow outside in winter.

## Core loop
1. **build** — the road pours out of the bakery like icing; gingerbread houses pop up along it; the cart rolls out of the door.
2. **load** — the oven dings and the treat (or passenger, or N cookies counted aloud) hops into the cart's tray. Tupi asks
   (voice + bubble).
3. **play** — the child drags the cart along the road (a tap on a house or road also drives there — for the youngest).
   - right house → brake, the door opens, the friend pops out, Tupi hands the treat over with her trunk, windows light, heart
     puffs; the cart drives back to the bakery for the next order.
   - wrong house → a soft bump, the friend peeks out and shakes its head kindly, Tupi names the house and repeats the goal, the
     cart reverses to the fork. Never a fail.
   - tap on the cart → Tupi toots and repeats the question (a tap is not a drive).
4. **party** — after the round's orders: Tupi trumpets and sprays a sprinkle fountain; stars fly to the HUD.
5. **wipe** — a seasonal swirl with flour sweeps across; a new town map appears.
6. After 4 rounds (or ≥ 3 once the chapter passed 75 s): **Talk time** — one question, 2–3 big picture choices + mic.
7. 4 chapters → celebration (all houses lit, confetti, Tupi sprays) → Play again.

Rounds per chapter (houses × orders): warm-up 2 × 1, then 3 × 2 at age 4.

## Stars
3 per round with no mistake, 2 with one, 1 otherwise; +1 for a right Talk-time answer. Assisted rounds ≤ 2★. Stars only go up.

## Kindness rules
No fail state, no timer pressure. After 2 mistakes the right road glows and the ghost hand drags along it. Hint cooldown 6 s;
idle nudges after 12 s, back off ×1.5 up to 30 s. The cart can never leave the road. Surprises (one round per chapter, never
the warm-up): a **mouse** peeks out of a flour sack (tap → squeak + wave), a **bird** on the window sill (tap → it sings), in
winter big **snowflakes** (tap → they twinkle). Never a mistake.
