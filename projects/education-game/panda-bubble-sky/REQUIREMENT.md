# Bao's Bubble Sky · Gấu Trúc Bảo Thổi Bong Bóng — requirement (2026-11-13)

Rolled with `/build-education-game random 4` (seed 2026-11-13, America/Los_Angeles): mechanic **balloon pop: tap the balloon
with the right answer** (family *pop*), theme **rainbow cloud land**, helper **a panda**, weather **spring → summer → autumn →
winter**, age 4 → colours · shapes · counting 1–5 · animals (homes). Recent families avoided: build, container, music, seek.
No swap: the mix is coherent (a panda blowing bubbles in the clouds through the seasons).

How it feels different from the last games (and from Kitten's Balloon Farm, the other *pop* game): no parade, no dragging,
no barn. The balloons become **soap bubbles that Bao blows with a bubble wand** — they wobble, drift on the breeze and
softly bounce off each other. A round can have **several right bubbles** ("Pop all the red ones!"). Every right pop frees
the thing inside and it **paints one piece of a rainbow** arching over the clouds; a finished rainbow shimmers, then Bao
blows a big **whoosh** and the leftover bubbles float away. The layout is a floating bubble field in the sky above a cloud
floor, Bao bottom-left on a cloud.

## Pitch
Bao the little panda sits on a cloud with a bubble wand. He dips it, blows — and big shiny bubbles wobble out, each with
something inside: a red ball, a blue rubber duck, a star cookie, a little cow. "Pop the RED ones!" Tap a bubble: *pop!* —
droplets sparkle, the toy inside zooms up and paints a stripe of the rainbow. When the rainbow is done, Bao goes "whoosh!"
and the rest of the bubbles sail away. The seasons change in the cloud land: blossoms in spring, sunshine in summer,
leaves and wind in autumn, snow in winter.

## Core loop
1. **blow** — Bao dips the wand and blows: 3–6 bubbles grow on the wand ring one after another, wobble free and float to
   their places in the sky field (buoyant spring, gentle bob, soft collisions). Warm-up round: 3 bubbles, 1 right one.
2. **play** — Bao asks (voice + bubble text). The child taps a bubble.
   - right → *pop!* (ring of droplets, flash), Bao names the thing ("A red ball!"), it zooms to the rainbow and paints the
     next piece. Count rounds count each pop aloud. More right ones left → "One more!".
   - wrong → the bubble squishes like jelly and bounces away (it does not pop); kind words name it and repeat the goal.
   - empty sky → a puff of tiny bubbles; Bao → giggles and repeats the question.
3. **rainbow** — all right ones popped → the rainbow shimmers, stars fly to the HUD, Bao cheers, then **whoosh**: the
   leftover bubbles float up and away.
4. After 4 rounds (or ≥ 3 once the chapter passed 75 s): **Talk time** — one question, 2–3 big picture choices + mic.
5. 4 chapters → celebration (Bao blows a sky full of bubbles, confetti) → Play again.

## Stars
3 per round with no mistake, 2 with one, 1 otherwise; +1 for a right Talk-time answer. Assisted rounds ≤ 2★. Stars only go up.

## Kindness rules
No fail state, no timer pressure. After 2 mistakes a right bubble glows and the ghost hand taps it. Hint cooldown 6 s;
idle nudges after 12 s, back off ×1.5 up to 30 s. Surprises (one round per chapter, never the warm-up): a little **bird**
flies by (tap → loop-the-loop + tweet), a **kite** drifts across (tap → it loops), in winter big **snowflakes** float down
(tap → they twinkle into tiny bubbles). Never a mistake.
