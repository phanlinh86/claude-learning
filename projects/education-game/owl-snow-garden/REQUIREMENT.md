## Pitch
Lumi, a little snowy owlet in a mint scarf, keeps a magic flower garden in the snow. The child drags big seeds into the
right flower pots and watches each one sprout and bloom, while Lumi talks, counts and cheers in English or Vietnamese.
The day passes as you play: pink morning, bright noon, orange sunset, and a starry night with dancing northern lights.

## Audience and learning goals
- Default age 4 (3–5 range). The parent picks the age (3, 4, 5, 6, 7+) in settings.
- Topics by age (4 chapters per session, one per time of day, easiest first):
  - 3: colours (2 pots) · big & small (2) · shapes (2) · animal friends (2)
  - 4: colours (3 pots) · shapes (3) · counting 1–5 · animal friends (3)
  - 5: shapes with mixed colours (4) · counting 1–10 · capital letters (3) · animal friends (4)
  - 6: capital ↔ small letters · first letter of a word · adding within 10 · counting to 12
  - 7+: first letter of a word · adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · odd & even
- Garden versions of each topic: "Plant the RED seed in the red pot", "Plant the STAR seed", "Plant 3 flowers!"
  (Lumi counts each one aloud as it blooms), "Plant a flower for the SEAL!" (arctic animal names: polar bear, seal,
  arctic fox, snow hare, reindeer, walrus, penguin, puffin, whale).
- No losing, no timers. Wrong drops hop back gently with kind words; after 2 misses the right pot glows.
- Stars only go up: 3 per round without mistakes, 2 with one, 1 otherwise; +1 for Talk time.

## Core loop
Lumi says the task (voice + bubble) → the child drags a seed (it swings on the finger) → drops it on a pot →
right: it hops in, sinks into the soil, a sprout pops up and a flower blooms with a chime, Lumi says the word;
wrong: it hops home, the pot wiggles "no" → round done when all seeds are planted → stars → next round.
4 rounds per chapter (a warm-up first), then **Talk time** (one picture question, answer by tapping or by voice),
then the sky changes to the next time of day. After 4 chapters: a night-garden celebration and "Play again".

## Voice
- Every instruction spoken and shown in a speech bubble; tap Lumi or the bubble to hear it again.
- Says the item's name on pick-up, counts aloud, praises with the child's name, never negative words.
- English (en-US) or Vietnamese (vi-VN, warm "bé ơi … nhé!") with the device's voices.
- Talk time: optional microphone answer (Safari speech recognition) — tapping always works.
