# Pip's Reef Tower · Tháp San Hô Của Bạch Tuộc Pip (education game, 2026-11-20)

Made unattended with `/build-education-game random 4` (seed 2026-11-20, America/Los_Angeles). The roller skipped the
families of the last 3 games (seek+container, memory+seek, trace) and rationed *container*. Rolled: mechanic **build a
tower: stack the right blocks; the tower wobbles with real physics** (family **build**), theme **under the sea**, helper
**an owl**, weather **sunny → breezy → rain → rainbow**, skills for age 4 **colours · shapes · counting 1–5 · animals**.

**One swap (sanity check): helper owl → a little octopus.** Owls don't live under the sea; an octopus with eight arms
that catches and steadies the blocks fits the theme and the mechanic, and no earlier game had an octopus helper.

## Pitch
Pip, a little peach-coloured octopus with a mint diving mask pushed up on her head, builds a shell tower on the seabed
for her fish friends. A golden starfish rides a little pink jellyfish high above the rock: "Let's build our tower all
the way up to the starfish!" Three shell blocks rest in the sand. Pip asks "Find the RED block!" and the child **taps**
it: a big bubble wraps the block, carries it up over the tower and pops — the block sinks slowly through the water,
rocking like a falling leaf, trailing little bubbles, and settles with a soft *plonk* and a puff of sand; the whole
tower sways gently in the water. Dragging works too. A wrong block bubbles up, bonks softly off the tower and drifts
home ("Bloop! That's the blue block. Let's find red!"). When the tower reaches the starfish, the starfish lands on top
like a crown, a glowing pearl pops up on every block as Pip counts "one, two, three!", Pip blows a stream of bubbles and
the pearls float up as bubbles and pop into sparkles while a school of little fish swirls around the tower. The weather
above the water changes each chapter: sunny (sun rays and dancing light) → breezy (a sea current, seaweed leans and the
tower sways, Pip holds it with her arms) → rain (drops dimple the surface, the water darker) → a rainbow above the waves.

## How it differs from the last games
Not tracing (Nov 19), not memory windows (Nov 18), not fishing (Nov 17). The gesture is **tap to send** (drag also
works); the layout is **a tower rising from a rock in the middle of a seabed**; the reward is **slow underwater sinking
physics, pearls lighting up and floating away as bubbles, a fish school**. Versus Koko's Cake Tower (same family, 6
days ago): a tap gesture instead of drag-and-release, water drag (slow leaf-like sinking with bubble trails instead of a
drop), shell blocks, a starfish crown and pearl-bubbles finale, sea animals with clue questions ("Who has big claws?")
and a puffer-fish surprise that puffs up when tapped.

## Audience and learning goals
- Default age 4 (3–5); the parent picks 3, 4, 5, 6 or 7+ in settings.
- Topics by age (4 chapters per session, one per weather, easiest first; chapters rotate between sessions):
  - 3: colours (2 blocks to choose from) · shapes (2) · big & small blocks · sea animals (2)
  - 4: colours (3) · shapes (3, different colours) · counting 1–5 (pearl dots; "count up" rounds stack 1, 2, 3 in order) · sea animals (3, "Who has big claws?" from round 3)
  - 5: shapes in mixed colours · counting 1–10 · capital letters · sea animals
  - 6: capital ↔ small letters · first letter of a word · adding within 10 · counting to 12
  - 7+: adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · first letter · odd & even
- A round = one tower: N blocks (age 4: 2, 3, 4, 4; warm-up round: 2 blocks, 2 choices), each a question.
- No losing, no timers. A wrong block bonks off and drifts home; Pip says kindly what it was and repeats the goal.
  After 2 misses the right block glows and a ghost hand shows the tap. Stars only go up: 3 / 2 / 1 per tower; +1 Talk time.

## Core loop
Blocks drift down into the sand → Pip asks → child taps (or drags) a block → bubble lift → it sinks onto the tower (the
tower sways) → Pip names it and counts → new blocks drift in → … → tower reaches the starfish: crown, pearls counted,
bubbles, fish school, stars → the blocks float away in bubbles → next tower. 4 towers per chapter, Talk time, then the
weather changes. After 4 chapters: a rainbow party with bubbles and fish confetti and "Play again". Surprise per chapter:
a sea current makes the tower sway and Pip steadies it, or a little puffer fish swims by (tap it: it puffs up round and
zooms off).

## Voice
Every instruction spoken (en-US or vi-VN) and shown in a speech bubble; tap Pip or the bubble to hear it again. Pip names
a block when it is tapped, counts the tower aloud, praises with the child's name, never negative words. Talk-time
question per chapter (picture choices for ages ≤ 5) with an optional mic answer.
