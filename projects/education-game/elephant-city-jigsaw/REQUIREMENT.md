## Pitch
Pom the baby elephant loves jigsaw puzzles. On Puzzle Street every shop window holds a picture with a few pieces missing —
you can see their dark shadows. Pom asks for one piece ("Find the RED piece!"); the child picks it up from the tray (Pom names
it: "The red car!"), carries it to its shadow and lets go — the piece is pulled in, clicks into place and sparkles. When the
picture is complete it comes alive, Pom trumpets a happy toot, and the next picture slides in. The street goes from spring
blossoms to summer sun, autumn leaves and winter snow.

## Core loop
1. A picture board slides in (spring), its missing pieces shown as soft dark shadows. The pieces tumble into the tray below
   with a staggered bounce, each slightly tilted. From round 2 one extra piece that does not belong waits in the tray too
   (ages ≥ 4), so the last ask is never free.
2. Pom asks for one piece (voice + bubble). The child presses a piece: it lifts (bigger, a shadow under it), Pom says its
   name. The piece follows the finger on a spring and swings like a pendulum.
3. Let go near its shadow (generous zone: +30 px sides, +90 px above) → it is pulled in, straightens, clicks, squashes and
   sparkles; counting pieces count aloud ("One, two, three balloons!"). The asked piece let go somewhere else on the board
   → it glides home and its shadow pulses ("It goes here!") — not a mistake; the next drop anywhere on the board snaps.
   A different piece let go on the board → it wiggles and flies home with kind words ("That's the blue kite. Let's find
   red!") — a mistake. Let go outside the board → it glides home quietly. A quick tap → the piece hops and Pom says its name.
4. Each ask fills one shadow (warm-up round: 2 shadows, later 3 at age 4). Picture complete → it comes alive (shine sweep,
   every piece does its own little animation), Pom cheers, stars, the board turns away and the next one slides in.
5. After 4 rounds (or ≥ 3 rounds and 75 s): **Talk time** — one question with 2–3 big picture choices + mic.
6. 4 chapters (spring, summer, autumn, winter) → celebration (all finished pictures parade past Pom, confetti) → Play again.

## Stars
3 per round with no mistake, 2 with one, 1 otherwise; +1 for a right Talk-time answer. Stars only go up.

## Kindness rules
No fail state, no timer. After 2 mistakes in an ask the right piece glows and a ghost hand drags it to its shadow. Hint
cooldown 6 s; idle nudges back off ×1.5 up to 30 s. Tapping Pom repeats the question. Surprises (one round per chapter,
never in the warm-up): a **pigeon** waddles along the pavement (tap → it flaps and coos), or **soap bubbles** drift up from a
bubble machine (tap → pop with a sparkle). Never a score.

## Talk time (per chapter)
Show one piece and ask "What colour is this car?" / "Which one is the triangle?" / "How many balloons?" / "Who is this?" with
2–3 picture choices (pictures for ages ≤ 5) and an optional mic.
