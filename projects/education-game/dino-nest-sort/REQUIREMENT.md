# Dino Nest Sort · Khủng Long Xếp Tổ (education game of 2026-10-29)

Made with `/build-education-game random` (age 4 default). Rolled ingredients:
mechanic **sort into baskets (drag items to the matching nest)**, theme **dinosaur valley**,
weather **sunny → breezy → rain → rainbow**. Swapped the rolled helper "a little bear" for
**a baby dinosaur** so the helper fits the dinosaur valley.

## Pitch
Bo, a baby dinosaur, has mixed up everything in the valley. The child drags eggs, shapes, fruit
and animals into the right nests while Bo talks, counts and cheers in English or Vietnamese.

## Audience and learning goals
- Default age 4 (3–5 range). The parent picks the age (3, 4, 5, 6, 7+) in settings.
- Topics by age (4 chapters per session, one per weather):
  - 3: colours (2 nests), shapes (2), big & small (2), animal homes (2)
  - 4: colours (3), shapes (3), counting 1–5, animal homes (3)
  - 5: shapes with mixed colours (4), counting 1–10, capital letters (3), animal homes (4)
  - 6: capital ↔ small letters, first letter of a word, adding within 10, counting to 12
  - 7+: adding within 20, taking away within 10, first letter of a word, odd & even
- No losing. Wrong drops bounce back gently with kind words; after 2 misses the right nest glows.
- Stars: 3 per round without mistakes, 2 with one, 1 otherwise.

## Core loop
Bo says the task → the child drags an item (it swings on the finger) → drops it on a nest →
right: it hops in with a boing, Bo cheers and says the word; wrong: it floats home, the nest
shakes "no" → round done when everything is sorted → stars → next round. 5 rounds per chapter,
then a **Talk time** question (answer by voice where supported, or by tapping), then the next
chapter and weather. After 4 chapters: celebration and "Play again" (new random topics/items).

## Voice
- Speaks every instruction, item names on pick-up, counts aloud, praises, uses the child's name.
- English (en-US) or Vietnamese (vi-VN) with the device's voices; text bubble always shown too.
- Talk time: microphone answer via the browser's speech recognition when available.
- Tap Bo to hear the instruction again.

## Platform
- iPhone and iPad (Safari, also "Add to Home Screen"); portrait and landscape; also works on
  desktop browsers. Hosted on GitHub Pages. No install, no network after load, no ads, no data
  leaves the device.

## Parent settings (behind a 2-second hold)
Age, language, child's name, sound effects, music, voice on/off, microphone answers on/off.
