## Pitch
Tuk, a round little penguin chick in a red-and-white knitted hat, plays on a frozen lake. Frosty **ice blocks** sit on
the ice, each with a picture frozen inside. Tuk shows them for a moment (the frost melts away), then the frost grows back.
The child **taps a block — or rubs it with a swipe — and the frost cracks off** with a crunch, Tuk says what is inside.
Two blocks that match **glide together across the ice and clink**, the ice melts into a little hole, and the picture comes
alive: a fish of that colour leaps out in an arc and splashes into the sea, a shape snow-sculpture slides to the shore,
N fish jump one after another (counted aloud), or the animal waddles off to its home. The weather turns from a sunny
morning to a breezy snow drift, a soft summer shower pattering on the ice, and finally a rainbow.

## How it differs from the last games
- Yesterday (Kiki's Camp Chimes) was tap/swipe a hanging chime that swings and rings: a *music* game, things hanging in a row.
  Here the board is a **grid lying on the ice**, the gesture is **reveal by tap or by rubbing the frost**, the skill is
  **visual memory + the topic**, and the reward is **gliding blocks + things leaping out of the ice**.
- Versus Dee's Forest Memory (11-01, also memory): no 3D card flip — frost cracks off / grows back on ice blocks; matched
  blocks *slide on the ice with friction* and clink; pairs are often **cross-modal** (numeral ↔ fish, animal ↔ its home);
  surprise rounds are icy (a block glides to a new place, a golden aurora pair, blocks drifting on ice floes).

## Audience and learning goals
- Default age 4 (3–5 range). Parent picks 3, 4, 5, 6, 7+ in settings.
- Chapters (one per weather stage, easiest first). A pair is two blocks that "go together":
  - 3: colours (same colour) · big & small (big ↔ small of the same animal) · shapes · animals (same animal)
  - 4: colours · shapes · counting 1–5 (numeral ↔ that many fish) · **animal homes** (animal ↔ its home)
  - 5: shapes in mixed colours · counting 1–10 · capital letters · animal homes
  - 6: capital ↔ small letter · first letter (letter ↔ picture) · adding within 10 · counting to 12
  - 7+: first letter · adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · odd & even
- Polar animals and homes: polar bear ↔ snow den, seal ↔ ice floe, arctic fox ↔ burrow, snowy owl ↔ nest, whale ↔ sea
  waves, puffin ↔ cliff hole, penguin chick ↔ pebble nest.
- Pairs per round grow gently (age 4: 2, 2, 3, 3); a peek of ~2 s at the start of every round.

## Rules (from the skill)
No fail state, no timer pressure; a mismatch slides back with kind words; after 3 mismatches in a row one pair glows and
peeks (hint cooldown 6 s). Stars 3/2/1 per round (only go up). Chapter limit: after ≥ 3 rounds, Talk time once the chapter
passes 80 s. Session ≤ 8 min. Every instruction spoken + in a bubble; tap Tuk to repeat. Talk time: one question per chapter
with 2–3 picture answers (+ optional mic). Parent gate (hold ⚙ 2 s): age, language, name, volume, sfx, music, voice, mic.

## Core loop
Tuk says the task → blocks slide in from the shore on the ice (friction glide, bump) → peek (frost melts in a wave) →
frost grows back → the child reveals a block (tap or rub) → a second block → match: both glide together, clink, melt,
reward leaps out / no match: both wobble and frost over again → round done → stars → next round. 4 rounds per chapter →
Talk time → next weather. After 4 chapters: a rainbow party with every friend and fish that came out of the ice.

## Voice
English (en-US) and Vietnamese (vi-VN). "Tuk" is spoken "Túc" by the Vietnamese voice. Names every revealed block, counts
fish aloud, praises with the child's name, never negative words.
