# Nuka's Snow Dig · Gấu Nuka Đào Tuyết — requirement (2026-11-23)

Rolled with `/build-education-game random 4` (seed 2026-11-23, America/Los_Angeles):
mechanic **treasure dig: tap the sand to find the answer** (family *seek*), theme **arctic**, helper **a little bear**,
weather **spring → summer → autumn → winter**, age 4 → skills colours · shapes · counting 1–5 · animals.
Recent families avoided: build, container, music, path, pop. No swap: a polar-bear cub digging in arctic snow fits.
Because Bun's Dino Dig (2026-11-08) used the same mechanic, the loop is made to feel different: the gesture is a
**rub** (swipe back and forth over a snow drift; a tap still works), the reward is a **float-up into the aurora** and a
**belly-slide**, and the layout has the aurora ribbon across the sky instead of a wooden shelf.

## Pitch
Nuka the polar-bear cub plays on a snowy field by the Arctic sea. Little things peek out of the snow drifts — the pompom
of a knitted hat, the point of an ice gem, the head of a fish, the ears of a plush fox. Nuka asks for one ("Find the RED
hat!"); the child **rubs the snow** on that drift — every swish is a big paw scoop that flings powder snow — and the thing
pops out. A right find twirls and **floats up on a sparkle trail into the northern lights**, where it glows in the aurora
ribbon. After each round the aurora ripples and Nuka does a **belly-slide** across the snow. The year turns from spring
(melting snow, flowers) to summer (midnight sun) to autumn (red tundra) to a winter night with snowfall and a bright aurora.

## Learning goals by age (4 chapters, easiest first)
| age | chapters |
|---|---|
| 3 | colours (2 hats) · shapes (2 gems) · big & small (fish) · animals (2 plush toys) |
| 4 (default) | colours (3 hats) · shapes (3 gems) · counting 1–5 (dig up N fish) · animals (3 toys) |
| 5 | shapes with mixed colours (4) · counting 1–10 · capital letters (ice blocks) · animals (4) |
| 6 | capital ↔ small letters · first letter of a word (toys) · adding within 10 · counting to 12 |
| 7+ | adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · first letter · odd & even |

## Core loop
1. Snow drifts pop up across the field (2–3 at age 3, 3–4 at age 4, up to 10 for counting), each with its clue peeking out.
2. Nuka asks (voice + bubble). The child rubs (or taps) a drift: each stroke is a paw scoop (2 scoops at age 3, 3 from age
   4), snow flies in the stroke direction; after a tap the paw keeps going slowly by itself.
3. The thing rises out of the hole and Nuka names it. Right → it twirls and floats up into the aurora ribbon; counting:
   every fish is counted aloud and the round ends at N. Wrong → the thing waves, Nuka says kindly what it is and what we
   look for; it stays in its hole (tapping it again is not a mistake).
4. Round done → the aurora ripples, Nuka belly-slides, stars; new drifts appear.
5. After 4 rounds (or ≥ 3 rounds and 75 s): **Talk time** — one question with 2–3 big picture choices + mic.
6. 4 chapters (spring, summer, autumn, winter) → celebration (every find dances in the aurora) → Play again.

## Stars
3 per round with no mistake, 2 with one, 1 otherwise; +1 for a right Talk-time answer. Stars only go up.

## Kindness rules
No fail state, no timer. After 2 misses the right drift glows and a ghost hand rubs it. Hint cooldown 6 s; idle nudges back
off ×1.5 up to 30 s. Tapping Nuka repeats the question. Surprises (one round per chapter, never in the warm-up): a **seal
pup** pops out of an ice hole (tap → it spins and splashes) or an **arctic tern** glides by (tap → loop-the-loop).

## Talk time (per chapter)
Show one item and ask "What colour is this hat?" / "Which one is the triangle?" / "How many fish?" / "Who is this?" with
2–3 picture choices (pictures for ages ≤ 5) and an optional mic.
