## Pitch
Rio the tiny dragon lives in a dinosaur valley where the eggs sing. "Listen to my tune!" — a spark flies to the red egg: *la!*,
then to the blue egg: *do!*. "Your turn!" — the child taps red, then blue: each egg wobbles, hops on its mossy nest and sings,
a seed-pod lantern on the vine blooms. Tune done → the eggs sing it back together in a wave and the little volcano puffs a
ring of sparkles. Counting: Rio taps the big spotted egg three times ("one, two, three!") — "Now you: tap it 3 times!".
Animals: baby animals peek out of their cracked eggs — "Quack! The duckling!" The day passes from a pink morning to a starry night.

## Core loop
1. Mossy islands puff in; eggs drop onto their straw rings, squash and settle with a gentle bob. From round 2 one extra egg
   that is not in the tune is there too (ages ≥ 4).
2. **Listen:** "Listen to my tune!" Rio blows a glowing spark to each egg of the tune in order; the egg hops and sings its
   note, Rio says its name ("Red!"), that lantern on the vine flashes. Taps during the tune are harmless (soft sounds).
3. **Your turn:** "Your turn!" — the child taps the eggs in the same order. The vine shows the tune as little pictures (ages ≤ 5);
   the next one pulses. Ages ≤ 5 also hear a soft name cue for each next note ("…and blue!").
4. Right → the egg sings brightly, a note flies to its lantern which blooms. Another egg → a soft "bumpf", it wobbles, kind
   words ("That's the yellow egg. Listen again!") and Rio replays the rest of the tune; after 2 misses the right egg glows.
5. Counting (ages ≤ 4): Rio taps the big spotted egg N times, counting aloud (number card on a stone tablet); then "Your turn —
   tap it N times!" Each tap is counted; the Nth completes it. No mistakes possible.
6. Tune complete → "Let's sing it together!" Any tap (or 6 s) → all eggs sing in a wave, the volcano puffs a sparkle ring.
   The eggs float up and away; next set.
7. After 4 rounds (or ≥ 2 rounds and 75 s): **Talk time** — one question with 2–3 big picture choices + mic.
8. 4 chapters (morning, noon, sunset, night) → celebration (Rio dances, all lanterns bloom, confetti) → Play again.

## Stars and kindness
3 per round with no mistake, 2 with one, 1 otherwise; +1 for a right Talk-time answer; stars only go up. No fail state, no
timer. Tapping Rio replays the tune. Surprises (one round per chapter, never the warm-up): a **baby triceratops** trots by (tap →
it does a happy hop and a tiny "rawr"), a **little bird** lands on the vine (tap → it sings back the last note); at night
**fireflies** come to listen (tap → they sparkle and zip off).

## Talk time (per chapter)
"What colour was the egg that sang first?" is too hard at 4 — keep the proven picture questions: "What colour is this egg?",
"Which one is the star?", "How many spots?" (numerals with dots), "Who says quack?" with 2–3 picture choices + optional mic.
