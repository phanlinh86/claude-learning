# Tumi's Flower Farm · Nông Trại Hoa Của Rùa Tumi

Rolled 2026-11-25 (America/Los_Angeles) with `roll.py 2026-11-25 4`:
mechanic **trace it: follow the glowing path with a finger** (family *trace*), theme **sunny farm**, helper **a baby
turtle**, weather **spring → summer → autumn → winter**. Avoided families: memory, path, seek. Recent: Mochi's Icing Bakery

## Pitch
Tumi, a little baby turtle in a straw sun hat, grows a flower garden on a sunny farm. "Let's plant a RED wavy row!" — the
child taps the red seed packet, then **follows the glowing dots across the garden bed with a finger**: Tumi's little
watering can rides under the finger, water sprinkles, and sprouts pop up right behind it and open into red flowers.
Shapes: "Which sign is the TRIANGLE?" → plant flowers all around the triangle bed. Counting: find the sign with number 3,
trace the 3 in the soil, then tap 3 spots to pull up 3 carrots, counted aloud. Animals: "Find the COW — moo!" → trace a
pebble path from the cow to the barn, and the cow walks home along it. Every finished garden blooms in a wave, butterflies
flutter up, and a prize rosette flies onto the farm fence. Seasons turn: spring blossoms, summer sun, autumn leaves on
the wind, winter snow (magic snowdrops still bloom).

## What feels different from the last games
- Gesture: **trace** (press and slide). Taps are only for choices and carrots. Last 3 games: flip / rub / tap.
- Reward: the traced line *grows* behind the finger (sprout → bud → flower), a bloom wave, a rosette on the fence.
- Layout: outdoors — a big raised garden bed in the middle of a meadow, signs / seed packets stuck in the grass below it,
  Tumi beside the bed, a wooden fence with rosettes, a hay bale with a peeking chick.
- Differs from Mochi's Icing Bakery (same family): outdoor farm, growing plants (not icing), a watering can, carrots
  pulled out of the ground (not chips placed), pebble paths, rosettes on a fence instead of a cooling rack.

## Learning goals by age (4 chapters, easiest first, one per season)
| age | chapters |
|---|---|
| 3 | colours (2 packets) · shapes (2) · big & small · animals → homes (2) |
| 4 (default) | colours (3 packets) · shapes (3) · counting 1–5 (find the numeral sign, trace it, pull N carrots) · animals → homes (3) |
| 5 | shapes (4, mixed flower colours) · counting 1–10 · capital letters (find + trace) · animals → homes (4) |
| 6 | capital ↔ small letters · first letter of a word · adding within 10 · counting to 12 |
| 7+ | adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · first letter · odd & even |
Tracing a shape / numeral / letter is the "write it" step; the choice before it is the "know it" step.

## Core loop
The bed is ready (fresh soil slides in) → Tumi asks (voice + bubble) → tap the right seed packet / sign (a mistake if
wrong) → trace: follow the dots from the glowing start; plants grow only where the finger follows the path; lifting the
finger keeps the progress → (count: tap N carrots up) → bloom wave + butterflies → a rosette flies to the fence → stars →
next round. 4 rounds per chapter (warm-up: easiest, fewest choices), then Talk time, next chapter.

## Rules
No fail state, no timer. Tracing is never "wrong": off the path the water just stops, Tumi says "Follow the dots!"; after
a while a ghost hand shows the way, and a struggling child gets "magic water" (the path grows as the finger moves anywhere
on the bed). Wrong choice → it wobbles, kind words ("That's the circle. Where is the triangle?"); 2 misses → the right one
glows and a ghost hand taps it. Stars 3 / 2 / 1 per round by choice mistakes (0 / 1 / ≥ 2), only up.
Surprises (one round per chapter, rotating, never the warm-up): **butterfly** — a big butterfly flutters across the sky;
tap: it loops and showers petals; **chick** — a chick peeks out of the hay bale; tap: "cheep!" and a puff of straw.

## Voice (EN / VI)
Every instruction spoken + shown in the bubble; tap Tumi to repeat. Names on every choice, counting aloud, praise with the
child's name. Helper: EN "Tumi", VI "Rùa Tumi" in titles, "Tumi" in lines (TTS VI "Tu Mi"). One Talk-time question per
chapter (picture choices + mic).
