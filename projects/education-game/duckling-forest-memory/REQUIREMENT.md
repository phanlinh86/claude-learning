# Dee's Forest Memory · Vịt Dee Lật Thẻ (education game, 2026-11-01 re-roll)

Made with `/build-education-game random 4` after the parents' feedback "the game keeps dropping something into a basket"
(same day as Kitten's Balloon Farm, so the roll uses `--salt mechanic=b --salt theme=b --salt helper=b --salt weather=b`
on seed 2026-11-01, America/Los_Angeles). The new roller skips the mechanic families of the last 3 games (pop, seek,
container) and rations *container* games. Rolled: mechanic **memory cards** (family *memory*), theme **outer space**,
helper **a duckling**, weather **spring → summer → autumn → winter**, skills for age 4 **colours · shapes · counting 1–5 · animals**.
One swap: the theme "outer space" became **fairy forest** (also in the theme table), because seasons don't happen in space
and a duckling belongs by a forest pond.

## Pitch
Dee, a fluffy yellow duckling in a little leaf cape, lives by a pond in a fairy forest. Magic cards lie face down on a
mossy clearing. Dee shows them for a moment, then flips them over. The child taps two cards to turn them, and Dee says
what is on each one. When the two cards match, they spin, sparkle and the picture comes alive in the forest: a butterfly
of that colour flutters out, a shape lantern lights up on a branch, acorns tumble out, or the animal hops out and stays
to watch. The forest changes season as you play: spring blossoms, summer sun, autumn leaves, then winter snow.

## How it differs from the last games
No dragging, nothing goes into a container. The gesture is **tap to flip**, the skill trained is **visual memory plus
the topic**, the layout is a **card grid**, and the reward is the forest filling up with life.

## Audience and learning goals
- Default age 4 (3–5 range). The parent picks the age (3, 4, 5, 6, 7+) in settings.
- Topics by age (4 chapters per session, one per season, easiest first). A pair is two cards that "go together":
  - 3: colours (same colour) · big & small (big ↔ small of the same thing) · shapes · animals (same animal)
  - 4: colours · shapes · counting 1–5 (numeral ↔ that many dots) · forest animals (two of the same animal)
  - 5: shapes in mixed colours (shape ↔ shape) · counting 1–10 (numeral ↔ dots) · capital letters (A ↔ A) · animals
  - 6: capital ↔ small letter · first letter (letter ↔ picture) · adding within 10 (2 + 3 ↔ 5) · counting to 12
  - 7+: first letter · adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · odd & even (numeral ↔ dots, card frames coloured odd/even)
- Grids grow gently: warm-up 2 pairs, then 3, 4, 4 pairs at age 4 (age 3: 2, 2, 3, 3; age 5+: up to 5–6 pairs).
- A **peek** at the start of every round: all cards face up for 2.5 s (age 3: 3.5 s, 6+: 1.5 s) while Dee names a few.
- No losing, no timers. A mismatch just flips back with kind words ("A fox and an owl! Let's find the other fox.").
  After 3 mismatches in a row, the two cards of one pair wiggle and peek. Stars only go up: 3 per round with few extra
  flips, 2 with some, 1 otherwise; +1 for Talk time.

## Core loop
Dee says the task → cards deal in from a stack with a flick → peek, then flip down in a wave → the child taps a card
(it flips with a 3D turn, Dee says its name) → taps a second card → match: both lift, slide together, spin and burst into
the reward that joins the forest; no match: a little shake, then both flip back → round done when every pair is found →
stars → next round. 4 rounds per chapter, then **Talk time** (one picture question), then the season changes.
After 4 chapters: a winter party with every friend who came out of the cards, and "Play again".

## Voice
Every instruction spoken and shown in a speech bubble; tap Dee or the bubble to hear it again. Names every card that
turns, counts the dots, praises with the child's name, never negative words. English (en-US) or Vietnamese (vi-VN).
Talk time: optional microphone answer — tapping always works.

## Sound
Loud and clear on phones (iOS media channel, compressor + makeup gain), with a volume slider in settings and big −/+ in
the pause menu. Card flip "fwip", match chime, sparkle, soft "hmm" for a mismatch, season music.

## Parent settings (behind a 2-second hold on ⚙)
Age, language (also on the title screen), child's name, volume, sound effects, music, voice, microphone answers.
