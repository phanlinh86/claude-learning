## Pitch
Bun the bunny explorer has found a sandy dig site in Dinosaur Valley. Little things peek out of the sand mounds — the tip
of a coloured egg, the point of a star stone, a toy duck's head, a bone. Bun asks for one ("Find the RED egg!"); the child
taps that mound and **digs** — every tap is a big shovel scoop that flings sand (keep tapping to dig faster; one tap is
enough, the shovel keeps going). The treasure pops out of the hole. A right **egg hatches a baby dinosaur** of the same
colour that hops onto Bun's treasure shelf; stones, toys and bones spin up and land on the shelf too. The valley goes from
morning to noon to sunset to a starry night with fireflies and a glowing moon.

## Learning goals by age (4 chapters, easiest first)
| age | chapters |
|---|---|
| 3 | colours (2 eggs) · shapes (2 stones) · big & small · animals (2 toys) |
| 4 (default) | colours (3 eggs) · shapes (3 stones) · counting 1–5 (dig up N bones) · animals (3 toys) |
| 5 | shapes with mixed colours (4) · counting 1–10 · capital letters (letter stones) · animals (4) |
| 6 | capital ↔ small letters · first letter of a word (toys) · adding within 10 (number stones) · counting to 12 |
| 7+ | adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · first letter · odd & even ("dig up all the odd numbers") |

## Core loop
1. Sand mounds pop up across the dig site (2–3 at age 3, 3–4 at age 4, up to 10 for counting), each with its clue peeking out.
2. Bun asks (voice + bubble). The child taps a mound: a shovel scoops (2 scoops at age 3, 3 from age 4); the mound shrinks,
   sand flies in arcs and settles. One tap is enough — the shovel keeps digging by itself; more taps dig faster.
3. The thing rises out of the hole and Bun names it. Right → reward (hatch / spin) and it hops onto the treasure shelf;
   counting: every bone is counted aloud ("One! Two! Three!") and the round ends at N. Wrong → the thing waves hello, Bun
   says kindly what it is and what we look for; it stays in its open hole (tapping it again is not a mistake).
4. 1–2 asks per round (warm-up round: one ask, 2 mounds). Round done → Bun cheers, the shelf items bounce, stars, the holes
   refill with sand and new mounds appear.
5. After 4 rounds (or ≥ 3 rounds and 75 s): **Talk time** — one question with 2–3 big picture choices + mic.
6. 4 chapters (morning, noon, sunset, night) → celebration (all treasures of the session dance around Bun) → Play again.

## Stars
3 per round with no mistake, 2 with one, 1 otherwise; +1 for a right Talk-time answer. Stars only go up.

## Kindness rules
No fail state, no timer. After 2 misses the right mound glows and a ghost hand taps it. Hint cooldown 6 s; idle nudges back
off ×1.5 up to 30 s. Tapping Bun repeats the question. Surprises (one round per chapter, never in the warm-up): a **mole**
peeks out of the sand (tap → giggles and spins), or a **baby pterosaur** glides across the sky (tap → loop-the-loop).

## Talk time (per chapter)
Show one item (an egg, a stone, bones with dots, a toy) and ask "What colour is this egg?" / "Which one is the triangle?" /
"How many bones?" / "Who is this?" with 2–3 picture choices (pictures for ages ≤ 5) and an optional mic.
