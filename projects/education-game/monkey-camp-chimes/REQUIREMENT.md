# Kiki's Camp Chimes · Chuông Gió Lửa Trại Của Khỉ Kiki — requirement (2026-11-10)

theme **mountain camping**, helper **a little monkey**, weather **morning → noon → sunset → starry night**, age 4 → skills

**Interpretation (no element swapped):** the *music* family was last used by Bop's Rainy Band (2026-11-05: tap band members on
stools, drum N times on a big drum). To make this game feel different the music is played on **camp wind chimes that hang
from a log beam** between two pine posts at a mountain campsite: the child **taps or swipes** a chime, it **swings like a real
pendulum** and rings its note; a correct note floats up and **lights a paper lantern** on the garland. When the round's song
is complete the child **strums across all the chimes** (one swipe) and they ring in a cascade. No drums, no stools, no song
sheet. The mountain campsite (tents, a campfire, pines, mountains) last appeared on 2026-11-03 with *seasons*; this one runs
through a *single day* (morning mist → bright noon → orange sunset → starry night with fireflies) so it looks different.

How it feels different from the last games: the gesture is **tap / swipe to swing** (no dragging — yesterday was a jigsaw),
the layout is **things hanging from the top** (a chime beam across the screen with Kiki and the campfire below), and the
reward is **a lantern lighting up per note + a strummed cascade song** with Kiki dancing.

## Pitch
Kiki the little monkey camps in the mountains and has hung a row of wind chimes from a log beam over the campfire. Kiki asks
for one note ("Ring the RED bell!"); the child taps the bell — it swings, rings, and a glowing music note floats up and lights
a lantern. Three notes make a song: "Now play our song — swipe across the chimes!" One swipe and all the chimes ring in a
shimmering cascade while Kiki dances. The day passes from morning mist to a starry night with fireflies.

## Core loop
1. The chimes drop in from the top on their cords one by one with a springy bounce and settle swinging gently. From round 2
   one extra chime that does not belong hangs there too (ages ≥ 4), so the last ask is never free.
2. Kiki asks for one chime (voice + bubble). The child taps it (or swipes through it): it swings away from the finger like a
   pendulum, rings its note, ring-waves glow. The **first chime a touch reaches is the answer**; other chimes the same swipe
   brushes just ring softly (never a mistake).
3. Right chime → bright note + sparkle, the chime name is said ("The red bell!"), a music note floats up and lights the next
   lantern. Counting strings jingle bell by bell, counted aloud ("One, two, three bells!"). A different chime → it swings
   with a soft wooden "bonk" and Kiki says kind words ("That's the blue bell. Let's ring the red one!") — a mistake (stars
   only). Tapping empty sky or the beam → a tiny beam wobble, no line.
4. All asks done (warm-up: 2 notes, later 3 at age 4) → **song time**: "Now play our song!" — a ghost hand shows a swipe;
   one swipe (or a tap on any chime) rings every chime in a cascade, the lanterns flash, Kiki dances, stars fly to the HUD.
   If nobody swipes for 4 s the song plays by itself. The chimes are pulled up and the next set drops in.
5. After 4 rounds (or ≥ 3 rounds and 75 s): **Talk time** — one question with 2–3 big picture choices + mic.
6. 4 chapters (morning, noon, sunset, night) → celebration (Kiki dances by the campfire, all lanterns glow, confetti of
   stars) → Play again.

## Stars
3 per round with no mistake, 2 with one, 1 otherwise; +1 for a right Talk-time answer. Stars only go up.

## Kindness rules
No fail state, no timer. After 2 mistakes in an ask the right chime glows and a ghost hand taps it. Hint cooldown 6 s; idle
nudges back off ×1.5 up to 30 s. Tapping Kiki repeats the question. Surprises (one round per chapter, never the warm-up): a
**hedgehog** trundles past the campfire (tap → it curls into a ball, rolls a little, uncurls with a sniff), or a **little
songbird** lands on the beam (tap → it sings back the last note); at night **fireflies** come close (tap → they twinkle).

## Talk time (per chapter)
Show one chime and ask "What colour is this bell?" / "Which one is the triangle?" / "How many bells?" / "Who is this?" with
2–3 picture choices (pictures for ages ≤ 5) and an optional mic.
