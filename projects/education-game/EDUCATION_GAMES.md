# Education games

Learning games for kids made with `/build-education-game` (one folder each). They play in Safari on iPhone and iPad
(portrait or landscape) and in any desktop browser, speak English or Vietnamese, and never punish mistakes.
`/build-education-game random [age] [hint]` rolls a new game (default age 4, i.e. 3–5) and adds a row here.

Tip: in Safari tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** to get a full-screen app icon. For a Vietnamese voice on iOS:
Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Vietnamese (download "Linh").
