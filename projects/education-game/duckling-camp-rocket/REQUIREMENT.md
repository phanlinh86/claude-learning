# Mimi's Splash Rocket · Tên Lửa Nước Của Vịt Mimi — requirement (2026-11-22)

Rolled with `roll.py 2026-11-22 4` (America/Los_Angeles date as the seed):
mechanic **rocket launch** ("tap the right target to fly the rocket there; collect the answers in order", family *path*) ·
theme **mountain camping** · helper **a duckling** · weather **sunny → breezy → rain → rainbow** · age 4.
No swap. Interpretation: the rocket is a camp **water-bottle rocket** (pump it, it whooshes up on a spray of water, pops a
parachute and floats down) — coherent with a mountain campsite and with a duckling who loves water, and different from
Bim's Rainbow Rocket (2026-11-02, a space rocket flying between cloud islands): new gesture (**pump** ×3), new reward
(parachute landing + pennants hoisted up the camp flagpole in order), new layout (launch pad + flagpole beside a meadow).
Recent games (families): music, build+pop, trace, memory+seek, pop+container, music+seek+container → *path* is fresh.

## Pitch
Mimi the fluffy duckling is camping in the mountains with her water-bottle rocket. Round picnic mats pop open on the
meadow, each with something on it. "Land on the RED one!" The child taps the right mat — the launcher swings round to aim,
a dotted arc shows the way — then pumps: tap, tap, tap ("one, two, three… blast off!"), Mimi stomps the pump, the bottle
fizzes, and WHOOSH: it shoots up on a spray of water, the parachute pops at the top and it sways down onto the mat
(in breezy weather the wind pushes the chute about; in the rain it splashes). The thing on the mat hops up and becomes a
pennant hoisted up Mimi's camp flagpole — collected in order. After the last launch the pennants flutter and are counted,
Mimi dances and the rocket shoots a celebratory fountain with a little rainbow in the spray.

## Learning goals (4 chapters × 4 rounds, easiest first, one per weather stage)
| age | chapters |
|---|---|
| 3 | colours (2) · shapes (2) · big & small · animals (by name) |
| 4 (default) | colours (3) · shapes (3) · counting 1–5 (pinecones on a sign; "1, then 2, then 3" in order) · animals & their homes (3) |
| 5 | shapes with mixed colours (4) · counting 1–10 · capital letters · animal homes (4) |
| 6 | capital ↔ small letters · first letter of a word · adding within 10 · counting to 12 |
| 7+ | adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · first letter · odd & even |

## Core loop
Round = k launches (2–4 by age/round). Each launch: m mats (2–3) unfold on the meadow, exactly one matches the ask →
tap it (aim) → pump ×3 (any tap counts; Mimi pumps by herself after 3 s of no pumping — never stuck) → flight, parachute,
landing → the thing becomes a pennant on the flagpole, the other mats fold up and fly away. After k launches: pennants
counted, Mimi dances, fountain. Wrong mat: it flaps and its thing wiggles, Mimi names it kindly and repeats the goal;
after 2 misses the right mat glows (auto-hint). No fail state, no timer pressure.

## Stars
3 per round without mistakes, 2 with one, 1 otherwise (an assisted round ≤ 2). Stars only go up.

## Voice (EN / VI)
Every instruction is spoken and shown in a bubble; tap Mimi to repeat. Pumps counted aloud ("One! Two! Three! Blast off!" /
"Một! Hai! Ba! Bay nào!"), landing praise names the thing, the child's name in praise, never negative words.

## Talk time (once per chapter)
Show a thing, ask a question (voice + text), 2–3 big picture choices (pictures/numerals with pinecones for ages ≤ 5) + mic.

## Surprises (one round per chapter, never the warm-up)
A marmot pops out of its burrow and waves (tap: it whistles) · a mountain goat hops onto the far rock (tap: it bleats and
hops) · in the rainbow chapter a little bird lands on the flagpole (tap: it sings).

## Parent settings (hold the gear 2 s)
Age 3–7+, language EN/VI, child's name, sound, music, voice, microphone, volume. Language toggle on the title too.
