# Bim's Rainbow Rocket · Tên Lửa Cầu Vồng Của Voi Bim (education game, 2026-11-02)

Made unattended with `/build-education-game random 4` (seed 2026-11-02, America/Los_Angeles). The roller skipped the
families of the last 3 games (pop, memory, seek+container) and rationed *container*. Rolled: mechanic **rocket launch: tap
the right planet/star to fly the rocket there; collect the answers in order** (family **path**), theme **rainbow cloud
land**, helper **a baby elephant**, weather **morning → noon → sunset → starry night**, skills for age 4 **colours · shapes
· counting 1–5 · animals**. No swap: a little rocket climbing through rainbow clouds from morning into the starry night is
one coherent story (the time of day is the altitude).

## Pitch
Bim, a baby elephant in pilot goggles, flies a little rocket up through Rainbow Cloud Land. Above the rocket float puffy
cloud islands, each carrying something: a colour, a shape, some stars, a friendly animal. Bim asks "Fly to the RED
cloud!" and the child taps (or drags a flight line from the rocket to) the right island: the rocket rumbles, blasts off on
a curving flight with a rainbow trail, lands with a bounce and collects the treasure into its little cloud wagon. The
camera climbs with every hop. After each round the child taps the rocket for a BIG BOOST through a cloud bank, and the
sky turns from morning to noon to sunset to a starry night where the islands become tiny planets.

## How it differs from the last games
No dragging things into containers, no popping, no card grid. The gesture is **tap a destination (or draw a line to it)
and then tap to boost**; the layout is a **vertical journey** (the screen keeps climbing); the reward is **a flight**: the
rocket arcs across the sky leaving a rainbow ribbon, and the wagon fills with what the child found.

## Audience and learning goals
- Default age 4 (3–5 range); the parent picks 3, 4, 5, 6 or 7+ in settings.
- Topics by age (4 chapters per session, one per time of day, easiest first; chapter topics rotate between sessions):
  - 3: colours (2 islands) · shapes (2) · big & small · animals (2)
  - 4: colours (3) · shapes (3) · counting 1–5 (count up hop by hop, then "the cloud with 3 stars") · animals (3, "Who says moo?" from round 3)
  - 5: shapes in mixed colours (4) · counting 1–10 · capital letters · animals (4)
  - 6: capital ↔ small letters · first letter of a word · adding within 10 · counting to 12
  - 7+: adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · first letter · odd & even
- A round = 2–3 hops (warm-up round 2 hops with 2 islands); each hop is one question with a fresh row of islands.
- No losing, no timers. A wrong island wobbles and Bim says kindly what it is ("That's blue! Let's find red.").
  After 2 misses on one hop the right island glows. Stars only go up: 3 per round without a miss, 2 with one, 1 otherwise; +1 for Talk time.

## Core loop
New row of cloud islands puffs in from above → Bim asks → the child taps an island (or drags from the rocket to it) →
right: ignition rumble, curved flight with banking and a rainbow ribbon, landing squash, the token jumps into the wagon,
the camera climbs so the island becomes the new launch pad → next question … → round done: stars, "Tap the rocket!" →
BOOST through a cloud bank (auto after 5 s) → next round. 4 rounds per chapter, then **Talk time** (one picture question,
optional microphone), then the time of day changes. After 4 chapters: a party on the moon with fireworks and "Play again".
One surprise round per chapter: islands drift on the wind, or a shooting star crosses (tap it for a sparkle shower).

## Voice
Every instruction spoken (English en-US or Vietnamese vi-VN) and shown in a speech bubble; tap Bim/the rocket or the
bubble to hear it again. Bim names the island tapped, counts the stars, praises with the child's name, never negative words.

## Sound
Loud and clear on phones (iOS media channel, compressor + 1.6× makeup gain), volume slider in settings, big −/+ in
pause. Rocket rumble + whoosh, landing boing, collect chime, boost roar, music per time of day.

## Parent settings (hold ⚙ for 2 s)
Age, language (also on the title screen), child's name, volume, sound effects, music, voice, microphone answers.
