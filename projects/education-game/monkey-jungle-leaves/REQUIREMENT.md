## Pitch
Lolo the little monkey lives in the Whispering Jungle, where the giant trees grow big leaves — and on the back of every leaf a
picture is hiding! Tap a leaf: it flips over and swings on its vine, and Lolo points and says what's there ("A red butterfly!").
Find the other leaf with the same picture and both leaves glow and grow a flower. Turn every pair and Lolo does a backflip — then
whoosh, she swings on a vine to the next tree.

## Learning goals by age (4 chapters, easiest first; one time of day per chapter)
| age | chapters (pairs) |
|---|---|
| 3 | colours (same thing, same colour) · shapes · big & small (the same animal big and small) · animals (two the same) |
| 4 (default) | colours · shapes · counting 1–5 (numeral ↔ that many dots) · **who eats what** (animal ↔ its favourite food: panda ↔ bamboo, rabbit ↔ carrot …) |
| 5 | shapes in mixed colours · counting 1–10 · capital letters · who eats what |
| 6 | capital ↔ small letters · first letter of a word · adding within 10 · counting to 12 |
| 7+ | first letter · adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · odd & even |
Topics rotate between sessions (≥ 5 per age, chapter 1 always the easiest).

## Core loop
1. The camera arrives at a tree. Its leaves flip over in a wave for a short **look** ("Look carefully!") and turn back
   ("Now they turn back!").
2. The child taps a leaf: it flips over and swings; Lolo leans, points and names the picture.
3. Second leaf: same (pair) → both glow, orchids bloom, a two-note marimba chime, butterflies flutter off, Lolo hops and says the
   pair ("Two red butterflies!", "The panda loves bamboo!", "Three — one, two, three!"). Different → both pictures wiggle, Lolo
   says kindly what they were ("A butterfly and a kite. Let's look again!"), the leaves turn back after a moment.
   An impatient third tap turns the two back at once.
4. All pairs found → the tree cheers (leaves sway in a wave, petals drift), stars, Lolo backflips and swings to the next tree.
5. After 4 trees (or ≥ 3 and the chapter passed 80 s): **Talk time** — one question, 2–3 big picture choices + mic.
6. 4 chapters → celebration (night jungle full of fireflies, every leaf glowing, Lolo dances) → Play again.

## Stars
3 per round with few mismatches (≤ max(1, pairs − 1)), 2 with ≤ 2 × pairs, 1 otherwise; +1 for a right Talk answer. Only up.

## Kindness rules
No fail state, no timer. 3 mismatches in a row → one unfound pair glows and lifts a corner. Idle nudges after 12 s, backoff ×1.5
up to 30 s. Tap Lolo or the bubble to hear the question again. Surprises (one round per chapter, never the warm-up, never a
mistake): a **toucan** lands on the vine (tap → "kraa!", it flaps off), a **frog** hops along the jungle floor (tap → "ribbit!" and a
big jump), a **blue morpho-style butterfly** flutters up past the leaves (tap → it spirals up with sparkles).
