# Lolo's Jungle Leaf Match · Khỉ Lolo Lật Lá Tìm Đôi

A memory learning game for children aged 3–7 (default 4). Lolo, a little monkey with a teal leaf bow, lives in the
Whispering Jungle, where the giant trees grow big leaves on long vines — and on the back of every leaf a picture is
hiding. The leaves flip over for a quick look and turn back again; then the child taps a leaf: it flips over on its stem
and swings like a pendulum, Lolo leans and points and says what is on it. Find the other leaf with the same picture and
both leaves glow in a sunbeam, an orchid blooms on each stem and a butterfly flutters off. Turn every pair and the tree
cheers (leaves sway, petals drift), Lolo does a backflip and swings on a liana to the next tree. The time of day changes per
chapter: misty morning → bright noon → orange sunset → starry night with fireflies.

Made on 2026-11-24 with `/build-education-game random 4` (see `REQUIREMENT.md` for the roll).

**Play:** https://phanlinh86.github.io/claude-learning/projects/education-game/monkey-jungle-leaves/game/

## Ages and topics (4 chapters per session, one time of day each; topics rotate between sessions)
| age | chapters (find the pairs) |
|---|---|
| 3 | colours · shapes · big & small · animals |
| 4 (default) | colours (same thing, same colour) · shapes · counting 1–5 (numeral ↔ dots) · **who eats what** (panda ↔ bamboo, rabbit ↔ carrot, bee ↔ honey …) |
| 5 | shapes in mixed colours · counting 1–10 · capital letters · who eats what |
| 6 | capital ↔ small letters · first letter of a word · adding within 10 · counting to 12 |
| 7+ | first letter · adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · odd & even |

## How to play
- Tap a leaf to flip it, then tap another. Same picture → a pair (glow + orchids + butterflies). Different → Lolo says
  kindly what they were and the leaves turn back. In a hurry? Tapping a third leaf turns the two back at once.
- No losing, no timer. After a few misses in a row one pair glows and lifts a corner. Tap Lolo or the bubble to hear the
  question again. Stars: 3 / 2 / 1 per tree, +1 for Talk time.
- Surprises (one tree per chapter, never a mistake): a **toucan** lands on a vine, a **frog** hops along the jungle floor,
  a **butterfly** flutters up past the leaves — tap them!
- After 4 trees: **Talk time** — one question with 2–3 picture answers (tap, or say it if the mic is on).

## On iPhone / iPad
1. Open the link in Safari and tap once anywhere (iOS only allows sound after the first tap).
2. Sound is loud by default and plays through the media volume; adjust it in settings (hold the gear 2 s) or with − / + in
   the pause menu.
3. Optional: **Share → Add to Home Screen** for a full-screen icon.
4. Vietnamese voice: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Vietnamese → download "Linh".
