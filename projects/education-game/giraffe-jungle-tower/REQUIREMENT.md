# Lulu's Jungle Tower · Hươu Lulu Xếp Tháp (education game, 2026-11-04)

Made unattended with `/build-education-game random 4` (seed 2026-11-04, America/Los_Angeles). The roller skipped the
families of the last 3 games (trace+path, seek, pop; memory too) and rationed *container*. Rolled: mechanic **build a
tower: stack the right blocks; the tower wobbles with real physics** (family **build**), theme **jungle**, helper **a
giraffe calf**, weather **morning → noon → sunset → starry night**, skills for age 4 **colours · shapes · counting 1–5 ·
animals**. No swap: a giraffe calf is the tallest friend in the jungle, so building a tall tower up to the bananas fits.

## Pitch
Lulu, a baby giraffe with a seafoam neckerchief, builds towers in a jungle clearing. A bunch of bananas hangs from a
branch high above an old tree stump — "Let's build a tower up to the bananas!" Three wooden blocks wait on the grass.
Lulu asks "Stack the RED block!" and the child drags it up onto the tower and lets go: it drops, lands with a *clonk*
and a little squash, and the whole tower sways and settles like a real stack of blocks. A wrong block boings off the
top and hops back home ("That's the blue one! Let's find red!"). When the tower reaches the bananas, the blocks light
up and count "one, two, three!", flowers bloom up the tower and a cheeky monkey swings down on a vine to grab the
bananas. The day passes from a misty morning to a sunny noon, a golden sunset and a starry night with fireflies.

## How it differs from the last games
No tapping hiding places (Nov 3), no rocket flight (Nov 2), no card grid, no popping, no catching into a basket. The
gesture is **drag UP and let go to stack** — the block drops by gravity onto the tower top. The layout is **a vertical
tower in the middle growing upwards**, the reward is **physics** (landing squash, sway of the whole stack, a count-up
glow from the bottom block to the top) and the round ends with **a monkey swing and a flower bloom**.

## Audience and learning goals
- Default age 4 (3–5); the parent picks 3, 4, 5, 6 or 7+ in settings.
- Topics by age (4 chapters per session, one per time of day, easiest first; chapters rotate between sessions):
  - 3: colours (2 blocks to choose from) · shapes (2) · big & small blocks · animals (2)
  - 4: colours (3) · shapes (3, different colours) · counting 1–5 (dots on blocks; "count up" rounds stack 1, 2, 3 in order) · animals (3, "Who says roar?" from round 3)
  - 5: shapes in mixed colours · counting 1–10 · capital letters · animals
  - 6: capital ↔ small letters · first letter of a word · adding within 10 · counting to 12
  - 7+: adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · first letter · odd & even
- A round = one tower: N blocks to stack (age 4: 2, 3, 4, 4; warm-up round: 2 blocks, 2 choices), each a question.
- No losing, no timers. A wrong block bounces off and hops home; Lulu says kindly what it was and repeats the goal.
  After 2 misses the right block glows and a ghost hand shows the drag. Stars only go up: 3 / 2 / 1 per tower; +1 Talk time.

## Core loop
Blocks drop onto the grass → Lulu asks → child drags a block up and lets go over the tower → it falls and lands (the
tower sways) → Lulu names it and counts the tower → new choices drop in → … → tower reaches the bananas: count-up
glow, flowers, monkey grabs the bananas, stars → blocks hop away → next tower. 4 towers per chapter, Talk time, then
the time of day changes. After 4 chapters: a starry-night party with fireflies and "Play again". Surprise per chapter:
a breeze makes the tower sway and Lulu steadies it with her head, or a butterfly lands on the tower (tap it).

## Voice
Every instruction spoken (en-US or vi-VN) and shown in a speech bubble; tap Lulu or the bubble to hear it again. Lulu
names a block when it is picked up, counts the tower aloud, praises with the child's name, never negative words.

ambience, music per time of day.
