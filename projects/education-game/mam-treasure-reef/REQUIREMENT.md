# Mầm's Treasure Reef · Mầm Tìm Kho Báu

**Made for:** Mầm, a quick, extroverted Vietnamese girl (default age 4; parents can set 3–7+). She gets bored fast,
loves funny sounds and faces, and loves to be encouraged — by name, pronounced properly ("Mầm", not "Mam").

**Rolled ingredients (2026-10-31, re-roll r1):** mechanic *treasure dig: tap the sand to find the answer* · theme *under the
sea* · helper *a little monkey* → swapped for **a baby sea turtle** (a monkey under water is incoherent; one swap allowed) ·
day cycle *morning → noon → sunset → starry night*. Extended with the drag-sort mechanic of the earlier games so the two
mechanics alternate chapter by chapter (variety = no boredom).

## Pitch
Tutu, a sandy baby sea turtle, has found a reef full of buried treasure. Mầm helps Tutu **dig** (tap the sand mound with the
right shell/pearl/sea friend poking out — *poof!* it pops out and flies into the treasure chest) and **sort** (drag treasures
into the matching chests). The reef is a map of **6 lagoons**; each lagoon is 4 short chapters (morning, noon, sunset,
night) of 4 rounds plus a Talk-time question. Finishing a lagoon opens the next one and adds a trophy to Mầm's **Treasure
Room**. All together ≈ 30–40 minutes of play, saved as you go.

## Core loop
- **Dig round:** 4–6 sand mounds on the sea floor, each with a treasure poking out (colour/shape/animal/letter visible).
  Tutu asks ("Dig up the red shell!"). Tap the right mound → sand poof, item pops out with a funny *boing* and arcs into
  the chest; wrong mound → wiggle, the item peeks fully, Tutu names it kindly ("That's a blue shell!"), it sinks back.
  2–3 targets per round (warm-up: 1). Count rounds: "Dig up 3 pearls!" (N+2 pearl mounds, count aloud). One mound per
  round may hide a giggling crab — a free surprise, never a mistake.
- **Drag round:** treasures rest on the sand; drag each into its chest (colour/shape/size/letter chests, a long chest with N
  slots for counting, animal-badge chests in "ask" mode). Physical motion (spring drag, arc, squash); tap ≠ drop.
- No fail state; after 2 misses the right target glows; stars 3/2/1 per round, only up. Talk time ends each chapter.
- **Funny:** silly synth sounds (boing, kazoo, bubbles, slide whistle), a photobombing fish every few correct answers,
  Tutu's dance at chapter end, confetti and a fanfare per lagoon.

## Profiles, score, progress
- Profile screen after the title: up to 4 profiles, each with a name, age and a sea-creature avatar. A profile **Mầm, 4,
  turtle avatar** exists by default so she can tap and play at once. Adding/editing a profile is behind the parent gate.
- Saved per profile: stars per lagoon per chapter (best), pearls (score: +1 per correct, +5 per round, +10 per chapter,
  +25 per lagoon), lagoons completed, play time, and the chapter in progress (quit mid-lagoon → resume there).
- Treasure Room: one trophy per lagoon (bronze/silver/gold by stars), total stars, total pearls.

## Voice
- Every instruction spoken and shown in a bubble; EN or VI; tap Tutu to repeat. Praise uses the child's name; the name
  "Mầm" is always spoken by a Vietnamese voice (even in English mode), falling back to a parent-set "say it as" spelling.
- Optional speech answers in Talk time; tap always works.
- Volume slider in settings (default 100 %) plus big +/− buttons in the pause menu; sounds mastered loud with a compressor.

## Platform
iPhone and iPad Safari (portrait + landscape, safe areas, Add to Home Screen), also desktop browsers. GitHub Pages. No
network after load, no data leaves the device.
