# Visual QA report — Mầm's Treasure Reef (2026-11-01)

Captured with `node tests/visual/capture.js` (Playwright/Chromium, `?mute=1&novoice=1&profile=test&seed=5`) on 4 viewports
(iPhone 393×852 @3, iPhone 852×393 @3, iPad 820×1180 @2, iPad 1180×820 @2) × en/vi: title, profile picker (2 profiles),
reef map (lagoon 1 gold, lagoon 2 in progress + resume, rest locked), treasure room, settings, profile editor, chapter banner,
dig / drag / count (long chest) / animals-ask / letters (age 5, drag + dig) / dig-count rounds, pause, Talk time (ages 3, 5, 7),
lagoon done, map after. Plus 4 times of day (dig + drag, en, age 5), notch/home-indicator insets (`--sa-t:59px; --sa-b:34px`
portrait, `--sa-l/r:59px; --sa-b:21px` landscape) on iPhone P/L and iPad L. 266 PNGs in `tests/out/visual/`, contact sheets in
`tests/out/visual/sheets/` (`python3 -I tests/visual/sheets.py`), DOM/world geometry in `measure.jsonl`
(`python3 -I tests/visual/analyze.py`), item/background contrast in `python3 -I tests/visual/contrast.py`, colour-blind
simulations in `tests/out/visual/cvd/` (`tests/visual/colorblind.py`, `palette_cvd.py`). No page errors or console errors.

All numbers below are CSS px unless marked "logical" (720-short-side world units).

## P1 — none

Nothing is broken, clipped off-screen or unusable. Every button measured ≥ 44 CSS px (smallest: pause/HUD icon buttons 48×48
on the compact landscape phone, chips 48 high), every treasure item ≥ 100 logical px (shells 120–130, gems 110, tags 120, pearls
115), every mound ≥ 130 logical px wide (iPhone L 170 → 150 with insets), lock / trophy / badge SVGs all loaded
(`naturalWidth` > 0), app icons present and full-bleed (see "looks good").

## Looks good

- **Safe areas**: with the notch/home-indicator insets the HUD (`top: sa-t + 10`), bubble (y 133 on iPhone P), map top bar (y 69), resume bar (bottom 808 < 818), pause/talk panels and Tutu (x ≥ 73 in landscape) all stay inside the safe zone; the Dynamic Island / home bar overlays never cover a control (`sheets/notch.png`).
- **Bubble vs HUD**: no overlap on any device (bubble is shortened to `hud.left − 12` or moved below the HUD); no text clipped (`scrollHeight == clientHeight` everywhere), 2 lines max on phones, font 17 px (phones) / 30 px (iPads). Vietnamese prompts fit ("Mầm ơi, đào vỏ sò màu xanh lá lên nhé!").
- **Play area**: no item outside the packed band (max excursion 9 px = idle bob + jitter), no item overlapping another (centre distance ≥ 1.1 × size), none over a chest, the HUD or the bubble; mounds never overlap each other, the chest, Tutu or the bubble; buried items poke out above the mound and stay below the bubble.
- **Dig chest sign** readable on every device/time of day (shell / letter / pearl + need dots / animal sticker), long chest sign shows "5 ○○○○○".
- **Night**: play band stays lit; items get a glow; all 6 colours ≥ 2.4 contrast vs night water (yellow 6.4, orange 4.4, green 4.1).
- **Map**: locks render grey on locked badges, gold trophy on lagoon 1, stars under unlocked badges, resume ribbon text fits ("Chơi tiếp · Vườn San Hô · Chương 2" on 373 px, 2 lines, 24 px). No badge overlaps the top bar or resume bar on iPhone P / iPad P / iPad L (gaps ≥ 12 px).
