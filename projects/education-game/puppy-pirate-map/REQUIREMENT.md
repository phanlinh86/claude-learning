## Pitch
Mit the pirate puppy found an old treasure map — but the wind blew pieces of it into the sea! The pieces come floating back on
the waves. Mit asks for one ("Find the RED piece!"); the child scoops it out of the water (Mit names it: "The red boat!"),
carries it to its shadow on the map and lets go — the piece is pulled in, clicks and sparkles. A complete picture comes alive
(the boat rocks, the parrot flaps), rolls up into a scroll, and the next part of the map unrolls. The island weather goes from
sunny to breezy (palm trees bend), to a warm rain shower, to a rainbow — and the last scroll shows where the treasure is.

## Core loop
1. A map parchment unrolls on the board, its missing pieces shown as soft dark shadows. The pieces float in from the right on a
   wave and bob in the shallow water below the map. From round 2 one extra piece that does not belong floats too (ages ≥ 4).
2. Mit asks for one piece (voice + bubble). The child presses a piece: it lifts out of the water (drips), Mit says its name.
   It follows the finger on a spring and swings like a pendulum.
3. Let go near its shadow (+30 px sides, +90 px above) → it is pulled in, clicks, squashes and sparkles; coins are counted
   aloud ("One, two, three coins!"). The asked piece let go elsewhere on the map → it floats home and its shadow pulses
   ("It goes here!") — not a mistake. A different piece on the map → it wiggles and splashes back into the water with kind
   words ("That's the blue flag. Let's find red!"). A quick tap → the piece bobs and Mit says its name.
4. Picture complete → it comes alive, Mit barks happily ("Arf arf!"), stars, the map rolls up into a scroll and the next one
   unrolls.
5. After 4 rounds (or ≥ 3 rounds and 75 s): **Talk time** — one question with 2–3 big picture choices + mic.
6. 4 chapters (sunny, breezy, rain, rainbow) → celebration: the scrolls unroll into the whole map, the dotted trail leads to
   the X, a treasure chest pops out of the sand and opens (gold sparkles, confetti) → Play again.

## Stars
3 per round with no mistake, 2 with one, 1 otherwise; +1 for a right Talk-time answer. Stars only go up.

## Kindness rules
No fail state, no timer. After 2 mistakes the right piece glows and a ghost hand drags it to its shadow. Hint cooldown 6 s;
idle nudges back off ×1.5 up to 30 s. Tapping Mit repeats the question. Surprises (one round per chapter, never in the
warm-up): a **crab** scuttles sideways along the sand (tap → it waves its claws), or a little fish blows **bubbles** out of the
water (tap → pop). Never a score.
