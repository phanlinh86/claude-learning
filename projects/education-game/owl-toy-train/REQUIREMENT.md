## Pitch
Momo the little owl drives a wooden toy train around the playroom. Each round a train chugs in and stops at the toy
station: the child drags the right cargo into each car — red blocks into the red car, the star block into the star car,
three teddies into the "3" car, the cow into the barn car. When every car is loaded the child **pulls the whistle cord**
(tap the big whistle on the engine): TOOT TOOT! the train jolts off with its cars bumping on springy couplings, cargo
bouncing, steam puffing, and rolls out through the toy-block tunnel. The window shows morning → noon → sunset → a starry
night (with a star night-light and the engine's headlamp on).

## What feels different from the last games
- Last game (Bop's Rainy Band) was tap-only music; this one is **drag** with a **ride** reward: the child releases the
  train themselves with the whistle, and the train's departure (spring couplings, wheel spin, puffs) is the payoff.
- Layout: a horizontal train across the lower screen, cargo on the playroom rug above it; Momo rides in the engine cab.

## Learning goals by age (4 chapters, easiest first, one per time of day)
| age | chapters |
|---|---|
| 3 | colours (2 cars) · shapes (2) · big & small · animal homes (2) |
| 4 (default) | colours (3) · shapes (3) · counting 1–5 · animal homes (3) |
| 5 | shapes with mixed colours (4) · counting 1–10 · capital letters (ABC blocks) · animal homes (4) |
| 6 | capital ↔ small letters · first letter of a word · adding within 10 · counting to 12 |
| 7+ | adding within 20 · taking away within 10 · first letter · odd & even |
Animal homes: barn (cow, pig, sheep), pond (duck, frog, fish), tree (bird, squirrel, butterfly), burrow (bunny, mouse, fox).

## Core loop
Train arrives → cargo pops onto the rug → child drags cargo into cars (sort mode; count mode = fill the "N" car with N
teddies, counted aloud) → all loaded → Momo: "All aboard! Tap the whistle!" → child taps the whistle (or after 6 s it
glows; after 12 s Momo toots it herself) → the train departs → stars → next train. 4 rounds per chapter, warm-up first
(1 cargo per car), then Talk time (one spoken question, picture choices + mic), next chapter.

## Rules
No fail state, no timer pressure; wrong drop → the car wiggles, the cargo boings home with kind words; 2 misses → the right
car glows and a ghost hand shows the way. A tap is not a drop. Stars: 3 / 2 / 1 per round (0 / 1 / ≥ 2 misses), only up.
Surprises (one round per chapter, rotating): **hitch** — a toy bunny hops onto the last car for the ride (tap it: it
waves and giggles); **balloon** — a balloon tied to the caboose; tap it: it bobs and pops out sparkles + a star.

## Voice (EN / VI)
Every instruction spoken + shown in a bubble; tap Momo to repeat. Names on pick-up, counting aloud in count rounds, praise
with the child's name. Momo: EN "Momo", VI "Cú Momo" / "Momo". Talk time question per chapter.
