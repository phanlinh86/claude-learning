How to Play

Flap with Space, ↑, a mouse click or a tap. Keep flapping or the bird lands on the ground!

Every flap drops a treat. It keeps some of the bird's speed, so drop it a little before you reach the turtle.

Land a treat on a turtle's shell to score. Hit several in a row for a combo bonus!

Score points to travel through Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter.

Other keys: Esc/P pause · M sound · F11 full screen
